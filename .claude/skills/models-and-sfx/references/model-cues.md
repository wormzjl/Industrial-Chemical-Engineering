# Real-equipment cues by family

These are the three to five features that make each family read as the real machine. They come from the owner's
reviews and fixes: the vacuum pump redraw, the prime mover review G1-G5, T1, E1-E6, and the chimney. Check a model
against its family's cues before you show it. Reuse the named parts.

## Rotating machinery

- **Centrifugal pump (OH2, BB2):** a volute casing with its suction at the end or side and its discharge on top; a
  bearing frame; a coupling under a guard; a baseplate on a plinth.
- **Liquid ring vacuum pump:** a short, fat ring housing between thick port heads, with the suction flange on top; a
  vertical separator vessel with a 2:1 head beside it, the discharge on its top; a seal-water stub; an outboard bearing
  on its pedestal. It must not read as another pump-plus-motor.
- **Dry screw vacuum pump:** a long finned or ribbed rotor casing, a gear end, a silencer on the discharge.
- **Motor (LV, TEFC):** fins along the frame, a fan cowl, feet and a terminal box. **Large motor:** an octagonal stator, a
  top-hat cooler, a terminal box on the side.
- **Generator, 2-pole above 20 MW (turbo):** a long horizontal cylindrical stator (octagonal is fine) with dished end shields
  carrying the bearings; finned coolers on top; a small exciter housing at the non-drive end; an HV terminal box
  underneath; its shaft at the turbine's height. No black windows.
- **Steam turbine:** a horizontally split casing with its bolted flange row; a steam chest with the trip-and-throttle valve
  and governor housing; the exhaust nozzle. **Large (tandem):** HP, MP and LP casings in a row, a crossover pipe above,
  and a double-flow LP visibly wider than the MP.
- **Gas turbine, industrial or aeroderivative package:** an enclosure with louvre bars and roof vent fans; a filter house
  on the compressor end with filter rows (not a black face); an exhaust plenum box, a rectangular-to-round transition
  and a tall stack with an open dark top and a rim.
- **Gas turbine, heavy-duty frame:** an open casing with its horizontal joint flange; a ring of 8 to 14 combustor cans
  angled out round the middle; a narrower compressor in front; an exhaust diffuser widening into the plenum; the filter
  house on its own steel with an inlet duct (and silencer band) down to the inlet plenum; a stack about 2 to 3 times the
  package height.
- **Gas turbine with a steam coil (HRSG):** a coil casing taller than the turbine package; an expanding inlet duct from
  the exhaust; headers (or a drum) on top carrying WATER and STEAM; the stack rising from its far end.
- **Combustion engine, high-speed set (1500 rpm, 0.5 to 2 MW):** a compact V block with two inclined banks of head covers;
  crankcase doors along the lower side; a turbocharger on top feeding the silencer and stack; the radiator on the free
  end, its fans facing out behind spoked grilles.
- **Combustion engine, medium-speed set (500 to 750 rpm, 5 to 20 MW):** visibly taller and longer; a row of large
  crankcase doors; two turbochargers on large sets; exhaust manifolds along each bank; a raised radiator bank beside the
  engine with its fans facing up; a turning damper or flywheel rim at the free end.
- **Gearbox:** a split casing with fins and a breather; the input and output shafts at different heights or sides.

## Combustion and stacks

- **Chimney, steel stack:** slender (height about 20 to 30 times the diameter), stiffening rings, a platform near the
  top, an open top with a rim. **Brick or concrete:** tapered, with a corbelled cap and a steel liner showing at the top.
- **Elevated flare:** a tall slender stack with a tip and pilots, a knockout drum at its foot.
- **Fired heater:** a radiant box with burners underneath, a convection section above, a stack with a damper.

## Vessels and units

- **Drum:** 2:1 heads, saddles (horizontal) or a skirt (vertical), nozzles with flanges, a manway.
- **Column:** a skirt, platforms at the manways, caged ladders, a davit at the top. Leave large columns alone.
- **Air cooler:** tube bundles on a steel frame, fans under rings, a plenum.
- **Cooling tower:** fan cells with fan stacks on top, or a hollow natural-draught shell; a basin.

## Finishes that read wrong

| Looks wrong | Use instead |
| --- | --- |
| A flat black rectangle (it reads as a hole) | Louvre bars, filter rows, fins, a spoked fan grille |
| A squat fat cylinder as a stack (it reads as a silo) | A tall stack, a transition piece, an open dark top with a rim |
| A part floating with nothing under it | A duct, legs or steel down to what it feeds or stands on |
| A smooth tapered body (it reads as a tank) | A casing flange with bolts, sections of different diameter, appendages (cans, valves) |
| Every size drawn the same | Proportions and parts that change with the size class (high-speed and medium-speed, industrial and heavy-duty) |
