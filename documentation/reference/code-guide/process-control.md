## Wired controller boxes and DCS (0.22.0)

[Wired control and FE power](wired-control-power.md) owns `runtime/control` and `runtime/power`: boxes run native ideal loops, DCS edits/monitors linked boxes, sensors have stable human IDs, and the common pipe/rack framework lays data/power lines. Structural sensor installation/removal is immediate without a solver; measurements and loop plans remain on the 100-online-tick engine cadence. The turbine also has internal admission regulation, so an external speed-sensor valve is optional. Current fluid/accessory/control formats are 14/4/3; older format introductions below are history.


Sensor installation is a saved fact. A measurement and an accepted controller regime/opening are disposable state. The fluid worker regulates directly on its trial state, independent of GUI requests. There is no PID, tuning or actuator lag.

A bare valve is a fully open pipe. One direct pressure module enables upstream absolute-pressure control; one flow module enables mass or actual-volume control. Extra modules are rejected. The active set selects regulation, fully open or exact closure; unreachable targets reach an opening limit and report limited control. For a held opening, extra throttling head is q²/(2 rho A²) (u^-2 - 1), where q is mass flow. u=1 adds no restriction and u=0 closes exactly. The rest test (`NetworkReduction.atRest`) gives a configured flow valve its static drive forward, nothing back and nothing at a zero target, so an island with a flow valve rests when nothing drives through it; the rest interval reports the valve CLOSED.

Ordinary bottom outlets draw HC and water at equal volume rates, then the remaining liquid, then gas. Boot drains draw water, HC, gas. Boot HC outlets draw HC, then water above the body D/3 weir, then gas. Shared draws drive losses, velocity limits, transport and displayed flows. The end-inventory weir complementarity includes arrivals and competing drains; it does not alternate whole-stream water/gas modes. At a vanishing flow a LIQUID end holding both liquids draws what first fills its connection's volume under the equal-volume rule (`PhaseDraw.mixedShares`): equal volumes until the smaller liquid is spent, then the larger; both whole when together they fill less; half and half for a connection of no volume. So a trace film over a full liquid draws almost none of the film. Every end function of `PassiveStepSolver` (density, viscosity, solid share, suction flow, moles, mass, enthalpy, stream) takes the connection volume for it; `NetworkReduction.resolutionFlow` and `SolidEventIntegrator.mobility` read the same stream. A hydrocarbon trace in a water-full vessel is omitted from its step layout ([fluid-science.md](fluid-science.md), "Omitted traces").

`VesselGeometry` uses body bottom as height zero; boot interface height starts at boot bottom. The hydrostatic head of a LIQUID, OVERFLOW or boot port (`PassiveStepSolver.portHead`) stands over the body bottom, the vessel node's elevation (`VesselGeometry.head`); a boot's column is the line below its nozzle, carried by the connection's own column, and is not counted again. In a round vessel each of the two stacked layers (water lowest in a boot drum, else the heavier liquid) weighs its liquid and its volume share of the solids; a vessel holding solids and no liquid stands them as one bed. A prism keeps g·m_c·H/V. Percentages are height percentages. Measurements retain overrange; accepted empty vessels read zero. Local-volume flow is measured at local accepted P/T; unmeasurable samples remain unavailable. Opening a GUI replays a cached payload without solving or applying pending input.

Fluid facts schema 3 (`FluidFacts.SCHEMA`) appends, after main's schema 2 (pipe joints and rack lanes), a device spec's vessel geometry record (form by name, capacity, height, diameter, length, boot diameter and length; absent, a prism of the spec's volume and level height) and the `control_valve` control (target pressure, source by name, sensor UUID, revision, mass and volume targets); schemas 1 and 2 still read. Schema 4 (0.15.0) retires the basis hash and appends the basis revision ([fluid-runtime.md](fluid-runtime.md)). State format 22 can rebuild. Accessory facts schema 2 appends sensors and modular conversion markers. Old registry IDs remain, old pressure targets survive, and probe removal never recreates a converted probe. Corpus images for old and new facts are gated: `compat/fluid/schema-3/` (11 registrations, 3 vessels, 1 shaped vessel, 1 control valve, 2 laid pipes, 1 rack lane; `FluidFactsCompatTest` counts `shaped` and `controlValves`) and `compat/accessories/schema-2.snbt`. Loaded legacy shape reconciliation queues edits; registered shapes/controllers operate unloaded.
