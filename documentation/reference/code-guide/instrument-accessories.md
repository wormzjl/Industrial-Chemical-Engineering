# Pipe accessories and pressure and temperature gauges

Up: [code guide](README.md)

## Read this when

- You change a pressure or temperature gauge: its settings, where it mounts, what it reads, how a client gets the reading.
- You change how a pipe fitting (sight glass, shutoff, check or pressure valve, Y-strainer, inline pump or compressor) is installed on a pipe, removed with the wrench, or refunded.
- A gauge shows "unavailable", a stale temperature, or no needle.

The fittings' hydraulics (registry edits, checkpoint fields) are in [runtime-registry.md](runtime-registry.md). Their drawn shapes are in [equipment-geometry.md](equipment-geometry.md). Their screens and renderers are in [client-screens.md](client-screens.md) and [client-rendering.md](client-rendering.md).

## In one paragraph

A gauge is a passive dial a player attaches to a straight pipe or to a constructed reservoir's tap. It adds no node, storage, flash or limit to the network. Its reading is what the engine accepted: a pipe tap reads the accepted pressure at its datum through the accepted section losses and control heads, and the accepted flowing temperature; a reservoir tap reads the vessel's top or bottom pressure or its temperature. Readings go only to clients that request them, on the engine's presentation schedule. A pipe fitting is an item applied to a pipe block: the block, its entity and its fluid identity stay; the blockstate gains the fitting, and the registry queues the hydraulic change as an engine event. One pipe carries at most one accessory, gauge or fitting. Every attachment has an instance id in the server's accessory store, so the wrench or a break refunds it exactly once. Attaching and removing commit at once after validation; hydraulic edits and readings keep the engine schedule.

## Contracts

- **Mounts.** A pipe takes a gauge only on a straight, end or lone section (`SightGlassGeometry.supports`), and only when no fitting is on it. A reservoir's sockets are separate from its fluid ports and keep their ids when the layout refreshes.
- **Saves** (AGENTS.md, "Saves and data formats"). The gauges, fittings and slot revisions are facts: a later facts schema, a field from a newer build, an unknown type, kind, unit or mount name (no alias) or damaged facts refuse the world, `icheme_accessories.dat` left as it is. A field added later gets a default equal to the behaviour before it. Pending operations and the deadline are state: another state format or state that does not validate is dropped; nothing is lost, because only a commit shrinks the held stack or refunds an item. Corpus: `src/test/resources/compat/accessories/schema-<n>.snbt`, one image per facts schema; a facts field change adds an image.
- **Speed sensor** (W3, owner decision D27): `speed_sensor` (`SensorItem` of `Sensor.Kind.SPEED`, unit `REVOLUTIONS_PER_MINUTE`) mounts only on a steam turbine's coupling guard (`Sensor.SPEED_PICKUP`, at its output coupling, on the face the shaft leaves by). Its reading is the shaft speed the trains last decided (`InstrumentService.speedReading`, a read of engine state). Installing or removing it queues the turbine's governor binding (`ShaftTrains.Governor`); the upstream control valve with source `SHAFT_SPEED` then works as the governor ([shaft-trains.md](shaft-trains.md)).
- **Scales.** Endpoints are stored as absolute SI values. A display unit or a gauge-relative reference never moves them. Input must be finite, endpoints non-negative, span positive.
- **Readings.** Pressure: the accepted tap pressure, or UNAVAILABLE where the pipe is pruned or disconnected. Temperature on a pipe: the accepted flowing temperature within 100 ticks of the interval; after flow stops it keeps its last value as NO_FLOW. A reservoir reads its `FluidView.Tank` top or bottom pressure, or its state temperature. Samples and observations are ephemeral: after a load the first accepted interval gives the first reading.
- **Schedule.** A watch replays the cached sample (a read) and adds the device to `FluidPresentation.observe`, so its bucket publishes even while the island is idle. Nothing solves on a watch, a menu open or a block update. Ordinary block updates carry the static `Metadata` only.
- **Fittings.** `PipelineFittingItem` sets the blockstate, asks `FluidWorldAuthority.installPipeCutoff`, `installPipeValve`, `installPipePressureValve`, `installPipeFilter` or `installPipeMover` (a queued registry edit on the projected registration), or for an orifice plate `installPipeOrifice(id, axis, beta)` (the pipe's minor loss plus `Orifice.loss(beta)`, the pipe still passive), or for an automated shutoff valve `installPipeActuatedValve(id, axis)` (control `ControlValve.onOff()`, open; the pipe block's `SHUTOFF` value `ACTUATED`, derived from the control by `FluidDeviceBlock.body`, its `PRESSURE_CONTROL` false; an empty hand calls `FluidWorldAuthority.toggleActuatedValve`, refused while `ControlFacts.loopHolds` it), then records the fitting in the store. A rejected install restores the blockstate. The sight glass is visual only and edits no registration. Wrench removal: `FluidWorldAuthority.removePipeAccessory` (an orifice plate's with its K, `removePipeAccessory(id, plate)`, which takes it off the minor loss again; the refund keeps its ratio, `InstrumentService.item(Fitting)`), which restores a plain pipe and keeps pending bore edits; a filter's cake leaves as one queued recovery.
- **Registry first.** The accessory store is the fact and the block state draws it: a pipe the re-alignment places or restates takes the sight glass its fitting record holds (`InstrumentService.fitting`, `RegistryRealignment.sightGlass`), on its run's default axis (the record keeps no axis). A run that cannot carry one stays bare and the record stays for the break to refund. `InstrumentService.occupied` counts a pipe fitting.
- **Breaking.** A broken host refunds each attachment once (`recoverOnBreak`; a creative break drops nothing).

## Process control

**Process control:** process sensors and the control valve's modules; the contracts are in [process-control.md](process-control.md).

Sensors use `Sensor.Installed` (host/mount, UUID, revision and kind), `Sensor.Reading` (SI units, accepted tick and availability), and `SensorItem`. They have closed housings and placement ghosts; readings exist only in the process GUI. P/T probes share analog sockets. Horizontal drums have distinct top P/T sockets and separate body/boot level identities. Ordinary pipes retain one accessory; a Control Valve permits one P or flow module. `InstrumentHosts` snapshots its bounds-aware loaded index under `INDEX_LOCK`, and never loads chunks.
`InstrumentService` installs/removes structural sensors immediately on interaction, revalidates exact held stack, mount/revision, reach and permissions, and consumes/refunds once. The store's facts hold `sensors` and `modular_valves` tombstones; a store saved without them reads empty lists. Duplicate sensor instances or occupied mounts refuse damaged facts. Legacy pressure-valve registrations, including unloaded ones, queue equivalent probe/valve conversions once. Removing the probe never recreates it. A sensor on a valve's `valve_sensor` mount sets the valve's control by one registry edit, `PhysicalFluidTopology.Device.withControl` (`editSensorValve`), so a laid pipe keeps its joints and lane. An installed or removed flowmeter calls `FluidWorldAuthority.flowmeter(host, installed)`, which queues the host's registration at its next revision so its island recompiles the tap; a recovered host's flowmeter is only forgotten (`forgetFlowmeter`), since the host's removal recompiles. Static metadata is disposable and rebound from the store. Samples are disposable; temperature retains its last accepted flowing value/time as NO_FLOW.

## Converter accessories

- The liquid converter takes the optional level gauge at `ConverterDesign.gaugePlace` (`InstrumentHosts.LevelGauge.of` takes every shape with a `GaugePlace`); `TankRenderer` watches its node cell with the open tanks' cells and draws its gauge without a roof, its liquid's depth over its floor. The gas converter has none.
- The gas converter takes the pressure safety valve on its apex stub (`ReliefValveGeometry.of`, `ConverterDesign.reliefFoot`, the drum's rule factored into `placed`) and the gas blanketing valve on its head (`BlanketValveGeometry.of`, `blanketFoot`). Registration passes both through a refresh as a drum does. The accessory store's format is unchanged.

## Gas blanketing valve and pressure safety valve

- Items (`ModItems`, both `LiquidLevelGaugeItem(properties, tooltip)`, no per-item settings): `gas_blanketing_valve` (a drum's) and `pressure_safety_valve` (a drum's or a pipe's). `Instrument.Type` appends `BLANKET_VALVE` and `SAFETY_VALVE`; `Type.vessel()` (LEVEL_GAUGE and both valves) marks a fitting placed where its host's shape gives it, through the accessory request, never through `install`. `MOUNTS` adds `blanket_valve` and `safety_valve`.
- Places: `EquipmentInstruments` reserves two top flanges, F1 (the blanketing valve) and F2 (the safety valve): on a standing drum on its roof row (a drum 3 m across, whose two fall in one cell, moves F2 to the next free roof cell); on a lying drum on the crown line, F1 at the rear end and F2 at the front, a cell from every top nozzle and half a cell from every probe station (an end with no such station reserves none). Geometry: `BlanketValveGeometry`, `ReliefValveGeometry` ([equipment-geometry.md](equipment-geometry.md)). `InstrumentHosts.BlanketValve(bounds, port)` gives the drum its `BLANKET` port; `InstrumentHosts.ReliefValve(bounds, port, face)` its `RELIEF` port, or on a pipe the outlet face. The outlet faces the player who fits it (`InstrumentHosts.toward(look)`); `vesselFitting`, `fittingType` pick the place the player looks at.
- A pipe's safety valve is its only fitting (`Host.occupied`, `InstrumentService.occupied`); a drum's stands beside its other fitting. `Metadata.relief()`, `safetyValve()`, `reliefFace()`; `Store.relief(host)`, `removeRelief`, a second map beside the fittings (`fittings()` lists both).
- `InstrumentService`: a fitted valve is one queued registry edit (`FluidWorldAuthority.blanketValve`, `reliefValve`, `pipeRelief`; a drum that refuses keeps no valve); removal and recovery refund the item. `EquipmentStructure` keeps a fitted valve's set pressure and the safety valve's face across a layout refresh (the default face where the new layout takes that cell). `FluidDeviceBlock`: a plain pipe opens its screen only with a sensor or a safety valve on it.
- Drawing: `InstrumentRenderer.drawBlanketValve`, `drawReliefValve` (fitted under the world's light; a placement ghost, green when clear, red when blocked, while the item is held).
- Format: accessory facts `AccessoryFacts.SCHEMA`, state `AccessoryFacts.STATE` ([FORMATS.md](../FORMATS.md#saves)); the types `BLANKET_VALVE` on mount `blanket_valve` and `SAFETY_VALVE` on `safety_valve`; corpora `compat/accessories/schema-<n>.snbt` (`AccessoryStoreCompatTest`).

- `InstrumentWiring` (no blocks, nodes or collision): each transmitter's cable leaves its head through an Ex gland in the bottom M20 entry (`GLAND`) and drops in a drip loop (`DROP`) to the skin; a 20 mm branch (`BRANCH`) carries one light-blue IS cable on its strap to the baseline, a 25 mm perforated tray (`TRAY`) every cable joined beyond it, shown by count up to `TRAY_MAX`, to the service enclosure's side entry. `Finish(cables, tray)`; v runs in metres along each run, the textures repeat every `PERIOD`.
- Textures `textures/wiring/branch` and `tray_1` to `tray_6`, each with LabPBR `_n` and `_s` (`tools/pbr-textures/`, `WiringTexturesTest`); drawn by `InstrumentRenderer` on the effects' render path ([client-rendering.md](client-rendering.md), [effects.md](effects.md)). ## Manual mounts and factory instruments

`EquipmentInstruments` supplies physical probe locations and eligibility. Standing drums have P/T/body-level taps on a front roof row, reserving F1/F2 footprints for the blanketing valve and the PSV (see above). Horizontal drums have crown P/T/body-level taps and a separate boot level tap. Sphere P/T/level heads sit on supported top-platform mounts. Floating tanks retain grade-level P/T and their digital level mount beside the front service area; the mechanical liquid-level gauge remains optional.

Rotating equipment, columns, exchangers, kettles, air coolers and fired heaters accept no new manual equipment probes. Their accepted internal channels are in `BuiltInInputs`. Both cooling towers own inlet/basin T probes and a built-in basin level head, at the retained level location. Level means liquid plus retained solid volume, excluding gas. Ordinary pipe/control-valve accessory rules remain separate.

`InstrumentHosts.equipmentMount` and `Sensor.levelMounts` adapt this catalog for selection, reach, occupancy and bounds. Digital heads and analog gauges reuse `InstrumentGeometry`; protected process roots and surface leads come from `InstrumentWiring`. The renderer uses installed mount IDs, actual exchanger nozzle sizes and the selected detail profile; it never requests or computes process state. Removing a pipe's last sensor retires its socket through `ControlService.bind`, preserving the cable as an open end.

Installed instruments retain accessory UUIDs and human IDs. Factory channel UUIDs derive from physical equipment identity plus channel name, with no accessory allocation; locations are hover information and never identity. Missing/unsupported quantities are unavailable, not zero. Old installed facts are retained or recovered by the existing accessory lifecycle, rather than replaced by factory allocations.

## Installed sensors, IDs and optional gauge

`InstrumentService` installs/removes structural accessories immediately, including equipment mounts (owner exception to scheduled process inputs). Readings remain cached accepted process observations, published on the engine cadence. Installing a fitting never forces a solver. `ControlFacts` assigns a stable human `SensorId` backed by each sensor UUID; controllers select IDs, not coordinates. Sensor housings share the workstation/control-cabinet neutral metal, dark bezel, blue display and amber tag treatment with P/T/F/L/N identification (`ControlAssets`). `InstrumentRenderer.identities` reads synced host metadata for fluid/machine GUIs; `DeviceColumn.instrumentIds` wraps each stable ID with its measurement label, including body/boot distinction. This needs no sample or process update. Controller choices and disconnected-display handling are in wired-control-power.md.

| Item | Supported host |
|---|---|
| Pressure sensor | Straight pipe, control valve or equipment pressure tap |
| Temperature sensor | Straight pipe or equipment temperature tap |
| Flow sensor | Straight pipe/control valve; equipment uses its nozzle pipe |
| Digital level sensor | Drum, floating-roof tank or sphere; cooling-basin level is factory fitted |
| Speed sensor | No manual rotating-equipment mount; use the factory shaft-speed channel |
| Liquid level gauge accessory | Floating-roof tank only |

Actual mounts come from `Instrument`, `Sensor` and equipment geometry; a generic equipment face is not automatically a valid process tap. Pressure/temperature/level metadata survives equipment reconstruction. Accessory facts schema is 4; the compatibility corpus preserves older fittings and the optional tank gauge. Data socket registration follows the eligible host and installed sensors, with casing/cabinet mounts instead of attaching an equipment socket to its fluid nozzle. ## Terms

- **Host**: the block entity a gauge mounts on: a pipe, or a reservoir's node shell cell.
- **Mount**: one place on a host for one dial, with its tap and its kinds.
- **Fitting**: a pipe accessory that is not a gauge; its record is an `Instrument.Fitting`, which the store reads with `legacy`.
- **Watch**: a client's explicit request for a gauge's, sight glass's, tank's or mover's display.


## Optional tank level-gauge accessory

Instrument.Type adds LEVEL_GAUGE and tank_level is a validated logical mount. Instrument.Fitting replaces the store-local pipe-only Legacy record; store methods retain existing fitting handling and permit a tank fitting beside pressure/temperature dials. Metadata synchronizes the optional fitting record. LiquidLevelGaugeItem has no GaugeSettings/menu. InstrumentService validates/commits install/removal through queued/revisioned transactions, with shared refund and invalid-host recovery. New type reuses the existing record fields, facts schema1/state1; no alias/migration added. Full shared selection bounds are available without surfaces on the server.

## Traps

- The item id `pressure_control_valve` names the Control Valve. `FlowControl.PressureValve` (facts control `pressure_valve`) is the legacy valve: a saved one is converted once to a `ControlValve` (`InstrumentService.reconcileLegacyValves`, `bind`).
