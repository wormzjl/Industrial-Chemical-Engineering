# Pipe accessories and pressure and temperature gauges

**Process control (batch 2026-10-26-process-control, 0.14.1):** process sensors and the control valve's modules; the contracts are in [process-control.md](process-control.md).

Sensors use `Sensor.Installed` (host/mount, UUID, revision and kind), `Sensor.Reading` (SI units, accepted tick and availability), and `SensorItem`. They have closed housings and placement ghosts; readings exist only in the process GUI. P/T probes share analog sockets. Horizontal drums have shared P/T sockets and separate rear body/boot level pads. Ordinary pipes retain one accessory; a Control Valve permits one P or flow module. `InstrumentHosts` snapshots its bounds-aware loaded index under `INDEX_LOCK`, and never loads chunks.
`InstrumentService` queues sensor installation/removal to its 100-online-tick transaction deadline, revalidates exact held stack, mount/revision, reach and permissions, and consumes/refunds once. Store facts schema 2 adds `sensors` and `modular_valves` tombstones; old lists default empty. Duplicate sensor instances or occupied mounts refuse damaged facts. Legacy pressure-valve registrations, including unloaded ones, queue equivalent probe/valve conversions once. Removing the probe never recreates it. A sensor on a valve's `valve_sensor` mount sets the valve's control by one registry edit, `PhysicalFluidTopology.Device.withControl` (`editSensorValve`), so a laid pipe keeps its joints and lane. An installed or removed flowmeter calls `FluidWorldAuthority.flowmeter(host, installed)`, which queues the host's registration at its next revision so its island recompiles the tap; a recovered host's flowmeter is only forgotten (`forgetFlowmeter`), since the host's removal recompiles. Static metadata is disposable and rebound from the store. Samples are disposable; temperature retains its last accepted flowing value/time as NO_FLOW.

## Read this when

- You change a pressure or temperature gauge: its settings, where it mounts, what it reads, how a client gets the reading.
- You change how a pipe fitting (sight glass, shutoff, check or pressure valve, Y-strainer, inline pump or compressor) is installed on a pipe, removed with the wrench, or refunded.
- A gauge shows "unavailable", a stale temperature, or no needle.

The fittings' hydraulics (registry edits, checkpoint fields) are in [fluid-runtime.md](fluid-runtime.md). Their drawn shapes are in [equipment.md](equipment.md). Their screens and renderers are in [client-gui.md](client-gui.md).

## In one paragraph

A gauge is a passive dial a player attaches to a straight pipe or to a constructed reservoir's tap. It adds no node, storage, flash or limit to the network. Its reading is what the engine accepted: a pipe tap reads the accepted pressure at its datum through the accepted section losses and control heads, and the accepted flowing temperature; a reservoir tap reads the vessel's top or bottom pressure or its temperature. Readings go only to clients that request them, on the engine's presentation schedule. A pipe fitting is an item applied to a pipe block: the block, its entity and its fluid identity stay; the blockstate gains the fitting, and the registry queues the hydraulic change as an engine event. One pipe carries at most one accessory, gauge or fitting. Every attachment has an instance id in the server's accessory store, so the wrench or a break refunds it exactly once. Attaching and removing commit at once after validation; hydraulic edits and readings keep the engine schedule.

## Contracts

- **Mounts.** A pipe takes a gauge only on a straight, end or lone section (`SightGlassGeometry.supports`), and only when no fitting is on it. A reservoir's sockets are separate from its fluid ports and keep their ids when the layout refreshes.
- **Saves** (AGENTS.md, "Saves and data formats"). The gauges, fittings and slot revisions are facts: a later facts schema, a field from a newer build, an unknown type, kind, unit or mount name (no alias) or damaged facts refuse the world, `icheme_accessories.dat` left as it is. A field added later gets a default equal to the behaviour before it. Pending operations and the deadline are state: another state format or state that does not validate is dropped; nothing is lost, because only a commit shrinks the held stack or refunds an item. Corpus: `src/test/resources/compat/accessories/schema-<n>.snbt` (schema 1 written by 0.13.0, schema 2 by the process-control batch); a facts field change adds an image.
- **Scales.** Endpoints are stored as absolute SI values. A display unit or a gauge-relative reference never moves them. Input must be finite, endpoints non-negative, span positive.
- **Readings.** Pressure: the accepted tap pressure, or UNAVAILABLE where the pipe is pruned or disconnected. Temperature on a pipe: the accepted flowing temperature within 100 ticks of the interval; after flow stops it keeps its last value as NO_FLOW. A reservoir reads its `FluidView.Tank` top or bottom pressure, or its state temperature. Samples and observations are ephemeral: after a load the first accepted interval gives the first reading.
- **Schedule.** A watch replays the cached sample (a read) and adds the device to `FluidPresentation.observe`, so its bucket publishes even while the island is idle. Nothing solves on a watch, a menu open or a block update. Ordinary block updates carry the static `Metadata` only.
- **Fittings.** `PipelineFittingItem` sets the blockstate, asks `FluidWorldAuthority.installPipe{Cutoff,Valve,PressureValve,Filter,Mover}` (a queued registry edit on the projected registration), then records the fitting in the store. A rejected install restores the blockstate. The sight glass is visual only and edits no registration. Wrench removal: `FluidWorldAuthority.removePipeAccessory`, which restores a plain pipe and keeps pending bore edits; a filter's cake leaves as one queued recovery.
- **Breaking.** A broken host refunds each attachment once (`recoverOnBreak`; a creative break drops nothing).

## Terms

- **Host**: the block entity a gauge mounts on: a pipe, or a reservoir's node shell cell.
- **Mount**: one place on a host for one dial, with its tap and its kinds.
- **Fitting**: a pipe accessory that is not a gauge; the store calls its record `Legacy`.
- **Watch**: a client's explicit request for a gauge's, sight glass's, tank's or mover's display.


## Optional tank level-gauge accessory (feature 2026-10-26)

Instrument.Type adds LEVEL_GAUGE and tank_level is a validated logical mount. Instrument.Fitting replaces the store-local pipe-only Legacy record; store methods retain existing fitting handling and permit a tank fitting beside pressure/temperature dials. Metadata synchronizes the optional fitting record. LiquidLevelGaugeItem has no GaugeSettings/menu. InstrumentService validates/commits install/removal through queued/revisioned transactions, with shared refund and invalid-host recovery. New type reuses the existing record fields, facts schema1/state1; no alias/migration added. Full shared selection bounds are available without surfaces on the server.
