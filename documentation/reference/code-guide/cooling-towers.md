## Geometry and placement

`EquipmentShape.Kind` appends `INDUCED_DRAFT_COOLING_TOWER` and `NATURAL_DRAFT_COOLING_TOWER`. Both are open reservoirs. The shape's separate `TowerBank` defaults to `ONE` and validates 1–6 columns and 1–4 rows. `ProcessUnit.Bank` keeps its dry air-cooler series/parallel meaning. The constructor and server share validated shape/specification paths.

`CoolingTowerDesign` uses width 4, 6 or 8 m and custom fill 2–6 m for each fan cell; the default is half the width (2, 3 or 4 m). It repeats one fan, one fill box and one distributor at cell pitch, with four casing walls enclosing the fill and distributor through the fan deck. A shared metal basin, steel frame and bank access complete the structure; concrete is not used. Each tower has one water inlet at distributor height and one basin outlet centred 0.5 m above the base, without an internal riser. Makeup shares the inlet. Natural towers have diameter 40–80 m in 10 m increments; shared `CoolingTower.naturalHeight(double)` sets height to `1.3 × diameter`, giving 52–104 m heights and a default 60 × 78 m tower. `CoolingTower` derives nominal contact area and basin volume from these dimensions, with 2 m walls and 4/3 m operating depth.

Both designs use the same bottom-outlet builder. The shared `ShapeRasterizer` nozzle draws the entire neck from its root inside the wall to the flange at the connected pipe's size; there is no separate fixed 0.4 m stub. The natural inlet samples its shell band at the cell-centred nozzle elevation while keeping its external flange position. Each fan cell uses `ProcessUnit.axialFan`, an open metal cowl with a rotor and grille. The shared rotor builder places the animation pivot inside the hub so its owning shell cell exists below the top grille. Induced-tower legs run from the basin floor at `y = 0` to the fan deck. The square deck has an `Access.DECK`-thick annular solid clipped to the cell bounds. A cylinder of radius `0.35 × width` fills the cowl from deck to grille for collision, making the rotor/grille area solid; the cowl ring is registered separately.

Thirty-two continuous inclined concrete V-strut stations rise from the basin floor at `y = 0` through the rim to the shell ring, without vertical lower extensions. The clear air-inlet height is `0.14 × diameter` (5.6–11.2 m), so `shellBaseHeight()` is 7.6–13.2 m above grade and the packing, internal posts, distributor and inlet derive from that base. A concrete ring joins the struts; six internal steel grillage beams and nine posts carry the fill, with sections scaled by diameter. The shared `ProcessUnit.bar` overload omits buried end faces while retaining oriented collision boxes. The natural shell uses 8 m hyperbolic bands with about 4 m circumferential chords, uniformly scales chimney radii to 90% of their earlier values and keeps its throat at 75% of total height. Matching `Solid.hollowCone` bands leave the chimney air empty. `ShapeRasterizer` adds only cells touched by facets, nozzles or climbs and sorts the result; it does not scan/fill the entire bounding volume. `NaturalTowerRenderer` uploads one static chimney VBO above the derived shell base and draws it once; `chunkSurface` omits only those same facets, leaving support and ordinary-equipment quads intact. `EquipmentGhost` uses whole tower facets for its preview. Ordinary placement checks every occupied cell against world height and blocks. Existing refresh logic restores cells and ports when chunks load.

## Contact physics

`TowerTransfer` is Merkel's rating, because a fill runs inside every Newton residual and nothing may march along it. Ambient is fixed at 298.15 K, RH 0.5 and 101325 Pa; humidity ratio is `0.621945 pv / (P − pv)`. Moist air enthalpy counts from dry air and liquid water at 273.15 K. With `h_s(T)` the enthalpy of saturated air at the water and the air line rising `m cp / g` per kelvin of the water's fall, the fall solves `cp ΔT mean(1/(h_s - h_a)) = K/m`, the mean taken over the four Chebyshev points 0.1, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.9 of the fall. That is one false-position root (`root`, Illinois step) on the fall as a share of what the wet bulb and the air's capacity allow. A pinch (`PINCH`) stops a division by zero where the air reaches saturation. The conductance is `K = k A z (g/2A)^0.6`.

The evaporation follows a Lewis number of one: the air moves straight towards saturation at the water's mean temperature, which sets its humidity, then its temperature from its enthalpy. The outlet closes the water's mass and energy, and `dutyWatt` and `energyWatt` use `FluidThermodynamics` liquid enthalpies, preserving the network reference. No water is retained in fill.

The coefficient `k` fits a declared game calibration: 2 m fill, 2 kg/s/m² each water and dry air, 308.15 K return to 298.15 K outlet. A volatile immutable `Calibration` holds it with the wet bulb and the ambient air, and a model cannot borrow another package's. This is a game rating, not a packing-vendor performance claim.

Airflow balances signed quadratic resistance with `20 speed²` Pa fan pressure and humid-air buoyancy over effective chimney height, searched on flow|flow|, in which resistance is a straight line. Still air picks the sign, as warm water can hold both drafts, and reverse air counts as counterflow. Fan-off operation can retain passive airflow; cold water can reverse it. `targetSpeed` searches the same fill for the set point. Fan power uses the declared 2 kg/(s m²) full-speed air loading and is proportional to speed³ for a fixed tower, independent of the solved state. Bank contact is calculated at `returnFlow / cells` and scaled once by cell count. Droplet drift is omitted. Sensible surface contact remains while the basin is wet, even with zero spray; the basin itself has no evaporation or condensation.

The rating replaced an eight-segment marched counterflow fill, whose air sweeps were the wall-deadline blocker. `tools/cooling-towers/fill-forms/` keeps that fill, its 1840-point table and a measured correlation form; the batch's branch review has the errors.

## Conservation and hydraulics

`TowerExchange.at` is the canonical exchange for Newton, conservative reconstruction and accepted presentation (`readings`, `atRest`). It takes the mass mean temperature of the liquid water of the spray streams and keeps makeup separate. `PassiveStepSolver` includes net water and energy exchange in node residuals; `ConservativeTransport` applies the same terms to reconstructed inventory and emits an `ATMOSPHERE` boundary. Positive fill evaporation removes water and carried vapor enthalpy. Duty is diagnostic and is never booked again. The existing accepted conservation audit sees the resulting boundary totals.

`TowerExchange.liquidWater` decides what `PhasePort.SPRAY` admits: a stream whose liquid is water alone and stays liquid at atmospheric discharge, with no freezing-point cutoff, so 80 °C feed passes. Gas in that stream is not refused: it passes to the basin, whose open-vessel vent takes it, and the fill counts the stream's liquid water only. A junction with no liquid of its own is primed through (`InletPhase.priming`) when every feeder upstream gives such water, on the walk `InletPhase.held` also uses (`feeders`), so a line built with the basin empty starts by itself. Anything else closes the inlet through `closeBoundary`, which also stops a pump in the line and preserves upstream inventory. A trace an open vessel's vent takes whole is scaled as a component it does not hold (`Equations.kept`): a junction may omit that trace, and the vessel's row must not resolve it.

The inlet row discharges at atmospheric pressure at nozzle elevation, using the incoming stream's density. Both ends are air breaks: `PassiveStepSolver.boundaryAllowed` refuses flow out of a spray inlet and into a tower by any other port (`againstTower`). `LIQUID` basin outlets retain level head. The common inlet accepts return and makeup supplied by external piping; there is no built-in float valve. Existing open-vessel spill handles overfill without an overflow nozzle. Basin operating depth is 4/3 m and construction creates no water.

## Controls and presentation

Fan banks default to manual speed 1. Target-temperature control solves fan share for the water leaving the fill before basin mixing, independently of basin holdup, and saturates at its endpoint when unreachable. With no spray it stops the fans. Natural towers stay passive. Capability and target-limit statuses do not hold supported operation.

`PassiveStepSolver.Result.towers` and `PassiveIntervalSolver.Result.towers` hold accepted rates. `FluidWorldAuthority` copies these into scheduled `FluidView.Tower` snapshots. The compact device screen starts with inlet temperature, basin temperature and the tank snapshot's bottom pressure, and combines sensible/evaporative duty as cooling duty. It retains inlet flow, fill evaporation and fan power/speed, omitting ambient pressure, mass, net flow, airflow, separate makeup/supply rates, drift and phase composition. `CoolingTowerBasinRenderer` draws cached level snapshots with two triangles for a bank; a natural basin shares the model's facet count, frame and inner-wall vertices. The same display carries accepted fan speed for `MachineAnimation`. A lower `InstrumentGeometry.levelSockets` mount accepts the drum-style level sensor; valve linking is deferred. Menu opening does not solve contact. Server control edits retain existing geometry and inventory.

`CoolingTowerBasinRenderer` and the floating tank gauge use the same `LiquidRenderer` for their texture, translucent layer and vertices. Basin surfaces preserve the water material's opacity (currently about 25%), removing the earlier minimum of 160/255. `CoolingTowerBasin.height` retains the published level and a 0.205 m minimum height (5 mm above the floor), capped at the 2 m rim. The tank gauge keeps its existing readability minimum. Both still use scheduled cached displays after block entities; no new process calculation or payload is introduced.
