## Read this when

- A player places, breaks, rotates, names or configures a fluid block, or installs, toggles or removes a pipe fitting, and the network does the wrong thing.
- A drum, tank or sphere registers wrongly as a reservoir (ports, volume, level height, open flag), a constructed mover or heat unit registers wrongly, or a layout refresh moves or resizes one wrongly.
- Faces join that should not (or do not join): a laid pipe's explicit joints, a rack lane, a transition pipe; a block draws the wrong joints.
- An edit waits on an unrelated island, or a topology event changes stock (`Topology event changed stock`).
- A pipe, structure, shaft part, cable or controller comes back by itself after a chunk load, or is held (`fluid_realign …`, `/icheme devices held`).
- A whole pipe line is resized wrongly (`resizeLine`), or a queued edit tells its listeners the wrong change.
- A blanketing valve or a pressure safety valve is fitted, set or compiled wrongly.

## What it does

`FluidWorldAuthority` is the one object per server that owns the fluid world: the model of the network package, the ledger, the registry, the runtime, the presentation, the saved data and the gauges' `InstrumentService`. It is a façade: every public entry checks the server thread (`owned()`) and forwards to one of its parts (`Placement`, `ChunkBinding`, `ViewBuilder`, `Displays`) or to the plant code it still holds (running columns, stills, converters, heat units, shaft trains, exchanger lines). A block placement becomes a queued ledger event in `WorldTopologyLedger`; the event fences the islands it touches. `PhysicalRegistry` applies the event once those islands are aligned at its tick and replaces them with recompiled islands (`PhysicalFluidTopology.compile`, then `IslandCoordinator.topology` through `TopologyChange`). The block, block entity and menu are thin adapters that hold no fluid state. The registry is the fact: after a chunk loads, the world is set back to it (`ChunkBinding`, `RegistryRealignment`).

## Main flow

A block is placed, to replacement islands:

1. `FluidDeviceBlock.onPlace` (server only) → `FluidWorldAuthority.place(position, kind, facing, cutoff, pressureValve, filter, inlineMover)` → `Placement.place`: the next identity, the default control (`PhysicalFluidTopology.defaultMover` for a mover, a pressure valve's default), the largest bore of the pipes it joins (else 0.05 m); a generator gets a water spec and is placed off, every other kind nitrogen; a reservoir is placed empty with `fluid.reservoirVolumeCubicMetres`. The block entity binds to the identity. The other ways in:
   - a drum, tank or sphere: `StructureRegistration.register` → `placeVessel(node, ports, volume, levelHeight, open)`: one RESERVOIR registration at its node cell (its lowest inside shell cell) with a VESSEL port per nozzle, placed empty; the node cell's `EquipmentShellBlockEntity` takes the identity (`bindIdentity`); the authority never binds a vessel's node cell itself (a loaded cell whose identity differs is logged `UNBOUND`, the saved registration kept);
   - a constructed pump or compressor: `placeMover(suction, kind, ports)`, one SUCTION and one DISCHARGE port;
   - a heat unit: `placeUnit(sides, vessel)`, one `HEAT_SIDE` registration per passage and a kettle's shell, partners named, in one event; `removeUnit` removes them in one event ([heat-exchangers.md](heat-exchangers.md));
   - a laid line: `LineLaying.execute` → `lay(cells, diameter, hosts)`, one passive pipe per cell with explicit joints (a rack lane at its rack's position) and the new joints of the pipes it meets, one event; its blocks are placed with `FluidDeviceBlock.laying` set, so their `onPlace` registers nothing;
   - a fitting: `PipelineFittingItem` sets the pipe's blockstate and calls `installPipe{Cutoff,Valve,PressureValve,Filter,Mover}`, a queued edit of the projected registration; the wrench `removePipeAccessory`; an empty hand on a shutoff `togglePipeCutoff`;
   - a layout refresh: `StructureRegistration.reregister` keeps a vessel's or mover's registration by one queued `edit` (`Device.withPlacement`; a vessel also takes its new volume, level height and opening while `FluidDeviceSpec.sameCharge` holds).
2. `submit` → `PhysicalRegistry.submit` walks from the position through devices no island owns. It enters a neighbour only if the edited device joins it before or after the edit (`joined`, through each of its `contacts()`); it stops at an owned device, which fences that island, or at a device a queued event edits, which orders the new event after that one. A fixed boundary the walk only passes fences no island, except a boundary alone in its island of one (`standsFor`, `loneIsland`). `TopologyChange.fence` stops those islands slicing past the event tick; the ledger records the event. A respecified boundary is flashed once (`validatedStock`, in memory only).
3. Next tick: `FluidWorldAuthority.tick` → `applyPending` → `PhysicalRegistry.applyPending` batches the events of one tick and one dimension whose owners are aligned (committed tick = fence tick, no attempt in flight); a certified owner is materialised to the fence first, a held one accepts at its committed tick (`acceptAtFence`). A burst of 1024 unapplied events applies at once.
4. The batch compiles only the touched components (`PhysicalFluidTopology.compile`) and calls `MinecraftFluidRuntime.topology` → `TopologyChange.topology`: the affected islands are replaced with new ids in one change. Each vessel keeps what its original island committed, bit for bit (`Inventory.sameContents`), its geometry and elevation following the registry. A replacement of one original's structure (`PassiveNetwork.sameStructure`) carries that island's junction states (`withJunctionStates`, each buffer resized) and its last interval; any other starts with none. A new island starts at `fluid.initialIntervalSeconds` cadence, or the merged islands' largest.
5. `RegistryHost.committed(removed, recompiled)` drops the cached menu data of removed and recompiled devices, marks each recompiled device for its next bucket, re-keys the presentation, and redraws each loaded recompiled device (`FluidDeviceBlockEntity.refreshGeometry`, then its neighbours' faces).
6. `listen`: each `submit` tells every listener its `DeviceChange`s (position, device before and after; a moved device stands at two positions), after it submits ([assembly-service.md](assembly-service.md)).

## Contracts

- **Event cost is bounded by the touched neighbourhood.** An adjacent device that shares no flow path with the edit is not touched, so a block broken beside an unrelated (possibly held) network does not wait on it.
- **A layout refresh is an edit, not a replacement.** A refreshed vessel or mover keeps its identity, controls and contents; `PhysicalRegistry.apply` keeps a vessel's moles, energy and solids in its new geometry and elevation (no stream carries potential energy, so a move books nothing) and moves its chunk-index entry; the island's next interval start closes it to the new wall. `WorldTopologyLedger.Overlay.check` refuses an edit that changes a device's kind or dimension or skips a revision (`Stale device edit or a changed kind or dimension`) and a vessel edit that changes more than its geometry (`FluidDeviceSpec.sameCharge`).
- **A laid pipe joins only through its joints.** A hand-placed pipe and every other device has `ALL_FACES`; a laid pipe the faces its route uses; a face joins only where both sides have a joint (`mechanicallyConnects`). A rack lane is a laid pipe at `Position.lane` 1 to `RACK_PLACES` with its width `slots` (at most `RACK_WIDTH`) and face `points`, at most two joints, passive, no fitting; two lanes meet where their points coincide; a lane meets a plain pipe only if it is a `transitionPipe`. A walk that looks up `cell.offset(direction)` misses lanes: use `PhysicalFluidTopology.across`.
- **Drawn joints are the registry's.** `FluidDeviceBlock.connectedState` → `FluidWorldAuthority.drawnFaces`, `getStateForPlacement` → `placedFaces`: `PhysicalFluidTopology.builtFaces` on the projected registry, queued events included; an in-line machine draws as the mover it is, on its axis only.
- **A mover is constructed or in-line.** A pump or compressor without ports is refused; a constructed one occupies its node and port cells (`cells()`) and meets pipes only at its ports (`contacts()`, `meets`); an in-line mover is a horizontal pipe carrying its control and has no power switch.
- **A fitting edit composes.** `installPipe*`, `removePipeAccessory` and `togglePipeCutoff` queue an edit of the latest projected registration, so pending bore and setting edits are kept and rapid clicks compose; a fitting is refused beside a jointed sideways neighbour (`canInstallFitting`). A check-valve pipe stays a node of its run, which carries the reverse it forbids (`checkRestrictions`).
- **An island must hold a device.** `capture` refuses an island with no member and `PhysicalRegistry.load` refuses to restore one; so a join fences a lone boundary's island of one (`loneIsland`).
- **Registry first.** The registry decides which devices, structures, shaft parts, cables and controllers exist. A block never teaches the registry a device it does not hold; it only teaches what the registry lacks (a paint, a name, a frame) for a world saved before (`FluidDeviceBlockEntity.bind`, `restatePaint`, `restateName`; `StructureRefresh.restatePaint`). The quiet flag (`FluidDeviceBlock.registered`, `quietly`, `quiet`) marks block edits that follow the registry and never edit it.
- **Held devices.** `PhysicalFluidTopology.Device.held` is state, never a fact: a held registration keeps its contents and settings and joins no island; a device beside it names it on its status line (`heldBeside`); a column refuses to start with one inside. A device a running column holds (`suspendedBy`, `suspended()`, `port()` on `PORT_LANE`) joins nothing but still `mechanicallyJoins`; anything that walks the registry reads `suspended()` first.
- **A load reads only loaded chunks.** A load path checks `hasChunkAt` or `isLoaded` first; the drawn joints read the registry and no block. `server.execute` on the server thread runs the task at once.
- **Flowmeter taps are derived, not facts.** `PhysicalRegistry.flowmeters` is a transient set built from the saved accessory facts and set before `load`; `load` attaches the taps it compiles to each restored island before its first slice (`IslandCoordinator.attachObservationPoints`), and a certificate replay keeps them. Installing or removing a flowmeter (`flowmeter(host, installed)`) queues the host's registration again at its next revision, so the island recompiles on the engine schedule; `forgetFlowmeter`, for a host being removed, changes only the set; `flowmeterOn(host)` is what the assembly walk reads. A tap adds no storage or resistance and is no part of a pipe's identity or a certificate's signature; a failed reading makes the measurement unavailable, never holds the island. A sensor-configured valve is one registry edit through `Device.withControl`, which keeps a laid pipe's joints and lane (`InstrumentService.editSensorValve`).
- **A generator or void shared by several islands** reads its status from its lowest island and its flow from all of them.

## Laws and constants

**Re-alignment** (`RegistryRealignment.serve`, from `IChemE.onLevelTickPost`): a chunk load queues its registrations (`nextRealignment`), shaft parts (`nextShaftRealignment`), cables and controllers ([wired-control-power.md](wired-control-power.md)) and the structures whose box it touches (`StructureRealignment.realignLater`), each once until checked. Each level tick checks at most 32 parts within 1 ms, then at most 8 structures in the time left (`StructureRealignment.serveRealignment`). It never loads a chunk and never solves. A part whose block stands is restated; a free cell or another fluid device block gets its block placed quietly (the replaced block drops nothing); a rack lane's cell takes the frame its lanes hold; a structure is rebuilt to its record (`StructureRealignment.realign`); any other block holds the part, released when a later check finds its block standing. Outcomes NOTHING, PLACED, RESTATED, HELD, counted in `FluidRuntimeDiagnostics` and logged as `fluid_realign`. A held shaft part joins no train, which splits there; a held structure holds its shaft part (`StructureRealignment.holdShaft`). Operators: `/icheme devices held` (each held registration, shaft part, cable and controller with what stands in its cell, at most 20 lines) and `drop <id>` (a held registration leaves as a broken device, what it holds booked as removed, nothing dropped).

**Build facts** (`WorldTopologyLedger.Builds`): each line's paint (`LinePaint`), each one-block device's name, each structure's record (shape, paint, text, name) by origin, each rack lane's frame (`RackFrame`); each leaves with its device's applied removal (`keeping`).

**Device** (`PhysicalFluidTopology.Device.builder(id, position, kind, facing, geometry, control)` with one setter per field that differs from its default; `build()` calls the canonical constructor, which holds every rule). It may carry `Port`s (cell, face, phase port, `ConnectionRole`; at most `MAXIMUM_PORTS`): a reservoir's are VESSEL; a constructed mover has one SUCTION port at its node cell and one DISCHARGE port; a `HEAT_SIDE` at least one INLET (its first at its node cell) and one OUTLET; a vessel has at most one `BLANKET` and one `RELIEF` port, both on its vapour space. `Device.heat` is a side's `HeatUnit`; a side compiles as an inline filter does: one pipe `sideIdentity(id)` from its node to a junction at its outlet port's height, carrying `HeatUnit.heatSide()`, its nozzles 1 m stubs; a kettle's shell compiles with its `Pool`. An actuator's half of a link takes the bore and roughness of the pipe it joins (`half`).

**Pipe line** (static `line(at, start, limit)`, the pipeline tool's sneak-use): the pipes reached through mechanical joints (as built, whatever a valve, cutoff or switch says); straight through a junction, stopping before one it would enter from the side; an in-line mover belongs to no line; null past `limit`. `PhysicalRegistry.resizeLine(id, diameter, limit, largest)` queues one event in which each pipe keeps its length, roughness, fittings and spec; it returns the line's length, 0 for no pipe, -1 past `limit`, `LANE_TOO_NARROW` or `NOZZLE_TOO_SMALL`, queuing nothing on a refusal. The authority's limit is `LINE_LIMIT`.

**Blanketing and relief** (`FluidDeviceSpec.blanket` within `MIN_BLANKET` to `MAX_BLANKET`, default `DEFAULT_BLANKET`, refused on an open vessel; `relief` within `MIN_RELIEF` to `MAX_RELIEF`, default `DEFAULT_RELIEF`, above a blanketing valve's vent pressure, `reliefFace` a pipe's outlet face). `compile` puts `ControlValve.blanket()` on the run into a `BLANKET` port. `PhysicalRegistry.reliefs` → `compile(..., reliefs)` (`Relief`): the run out of the outlet carries `FlowControl.PressureValve(set)` stated from the guarded node; a far end with its own valve or a driving end gets a junction between two halves; with nothing piped, a stub `RELIEF_STUB` to a void at the ambient (identity `reliefIdentity(id)`). Entries: `blanketValve`, `blanketPressure`, `reliefValve`, `pipeRelief`, `reliefPressure`; `Placement.fittedRelief` starts above the vent pressure.

**Ledger** (`WorldTopologyLedger`): queued events (bounded), the online tick (`tick()` adds one), material totals on the component basis (`MaterialTotal.plus`: a crystal booked as its component's moles plus its energy offset; no potential energy), recoveries awaiting delivery, the column units, the converters, `Snapshot.heatTransits` and `withHeatTransits` (the lower-id side holds a pair's transit), `removedHeat` (a removed unit's transit, booked as destroyed energy). `FluidBasis`: package id, component axis and `revision` (`MaterialCatalog.fluidRevision`, at most `MaterialCatalog.MAX_IDENTITY` characters); `sameAs` compares the compound ids in order and the revision, the package id a label.

**Device spec** (`FluidDeviceSpec`): volume up to `MAX_VOLUME` (the same as `EquipmentShape.MAX_VOLUME`), `levelHeight` up to `MAX_LEVEL_HEIGHT`; a void's or generator's T, p inside the package envelope (`validate` → `FluidDomain.requireInput`).

## Formats

The registrations, specs, joints, lanes, heat units, held identities and build facts are facts or state of the topology unit ([runtime-saves.md](runtime-saves.md), numbers in [FORMATS.md](../FORMATS.md)). Enums are saved by name; `Cutoff` and `ConnectionRole` have stable codes. A device's name is block-entity NBT (`DeviceNames`), not a fluid fact. The block-entity update tag carries `FluidIdentity`, the gauges' metadata, the drawn `Bore` and `Faces`, a pipe's `Joints`, `Paint`, `Secondary`, a named device's `Name` and a transition pipe's `Offsets`; a vessel's shell cell keeps its identity out of its update tag.

## Traps

- **`recoverFilter` only queues.** It validates the registration, revision, kind and the player's free slot, and submits a ledger event with a `Recovery`; the engine applies it with the next batch, takes the cake and delivers the item; an empty filter or full inventory then is refused in the menu's next reply.
- **A fresh generator feeds nothing**: `place` registers a generator switched off (`generatorEnabled` false).
- **A held registration is not gone**, and neither is a device a column holds: their registrations and contents stay. A `port()` device is the column's, never the player's.
- **A chunk load marks its devices one tick late.** The load handler is a queued `TickTask` (`IChemE.onFluidChunkLoaded`) and a block entity's `onLoad` runs on the next level tick, so a GameTest that reloads a chunk at online tick v gets the view at the first bucket after v+1.
- **`FluidDeviceBlockEntity.released` runs only on the server thread.** A removal on another thread leaves the device marked loaded until its next bucket.
- **`UNBOUND: saved inventory retained`** is a live consistency status, not compatibility: the registry holds a device where the world now has another block.
- **The pump, compressor and filter blocks have no block item**: their item ids belong to the pipe fittings.
