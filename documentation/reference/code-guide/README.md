## Level 1 — Overview

Industrial Chemical Engineering (IChemE, formerly CreateChemE) is a Java 21 addon for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge; Create and KubeJS optional). It simulates chemical-process equipment with real thermodynamics. It has two process systems and an equipment layer:

- **Distillation column (V3).** The player enters a crude feed, a tray count, pressure, steam, side draws, pumparounds, a condenser and a reboiler. The column calculator then solves the coupled material, equilibrium and energy balance (MESH) equations with a damped Newton method on a log-coordinate state, from a learned (Transformer) seed or the classical initializer. A result is published only after the convergence and physical-acceptance audits pass. No block runs it on its own: one column solve (`ColumnSolve.calculate`) serves the process simulator's column on the client and a built column on the server, each with its own cold start.
- **Fluid network.** Pipes, tanks, junctions, pumps, compressors, filters and phase ports are placed in the world; the pipe laying tool lays a whole line along a searched route, on pipe racks where they stand, and a laid pipe joins only through the faces its route uses. A pipe can carry a fitting (shutoff, check or control valve, Y-strainer, inline pump or compressor, sight glass) and pressure or temperature gauges; process sensors (pressure, temperature, flow, level) report the accepted interval in the fluid screen, and a control valve with a pressure or flow module regulates inside the solve; a floating-roof tank is open to the atmosphere, so it vents its gas, overflows at its fill height and spills over its shell. Heat units exchange heat between streams: an exchanger's two sides through Standard or Low-dP crossflow routing, an air cooler against ambient air, a fired heater's radiant and convection coils, a kettle's tubes boiling the pool in its shell; each side holds its outlet by its own bypass, fans or burners, or works a real valve once its lines check complete. They form *islands*: connected sets that each own one solver. Each island advances in simulated online time (20 TPS nominal) with an implicit step: pressure-driven flow through pipe resistance (the 100 m/s velocity cap is part of each throttling line's law), phase layout in vessels, transport of solids and slurry, and a multiphase equilibrium engine. The engine runs tangent-plane stability with TP, PH and UV flashes on the PR78 kernel (E-PPR78 kij(T) for group packages), every phase at its own pressure, IF97 Region 1 water with its steam a second-virial gas (IF97 Region 2's B), and crystals (CO2-I, ice Ih) that form and melt in vessels, move as solids and melt on arrival in a junction above their melting point. A solve without a carried point starts from the island's quasi-steady balance. Leaving a data range never holds an island; the status line notes it.
- **Process simulator.** A block with a drag-and-drop flowsheet: feeds, units and streams, a sequential-modular run with torn loops on the client (never on the server), the V3 column as one unit, sizing of every unit and line against Must and Should rows with Auto/Manual equipment choices and a 20 % spare, and Send to block, which hands sized designs to the equipment constructor. The block only stores the sheet and results and lets one player edit. See [process-simulator.md](process-simulator.md).
- **Equipment.** An equipment constructor builds and deconstructs equipment; direct breaking requires Creative and other ordinary removal paths are blocked. It builds drums, distillation columns and process units (among them pump and compressor casings, a steam turbine, motor, generator and gearbox, each with its design point) whole on the block grid, drawn per cell in the chunk mesh with 1/8-block collision; drums, floating-roof tanks and spheres are fluid-network reservoirs with their design volume, level height and nozzle ports. Fully constructed pumps and both compressor models register one directed fluid device, a steam turbine one too; exchangers, kettles, air coolers and fired heaters register one heat side per passage (a kettle's shell a vessel); other columns/units remain drawing-only. A column is built only from a design the process simulator saved; with the units, valves and lines round it, it is an assembly that "Check parts" checks against that design and that reverts to not validated when a part it relied on changes. An exchanger whose control asks for a bypass or a ratio station is an assembly too. See [equipment-geometry.md](equipment-geometry.md), [equipment-designs.md](equipment-designs.md), [equipment-structure.md](equipment-structure.md), [heat-exchangers.md](heat-exchangers.md) and [machine-assemblies.md](machine-assemblies.md).
- **Rotating machinery.** A constructed pump or compressor runs on its own law: a centrifugal curve at the speed its driver sets, or a positive-displacement line with slip and a relief valve; a pump cavitates on an NPSH3 floor and takes gas, a compressor takes liquid. Shaft blocks and gearboxes join casings to one driver each (a motor on FE, a steam turbine with internal admission regulation, a liquid-fuel combustion engine or a gas turbine on its fuel line, a creative motor, or with Create a Create coupling). Drivers step once per slice from the power their machines booked; motors, generators and fans keep electric stores; pumps wear from cavitation and running dry and are repaired with a kit. See [rotating-machinery.md](rotating-machinery.md) and [shaft-trains.md](shaft-trains.md).

Both systems share a material catalog loaded from the datapack (components, datasets, methods, packages, presets) and a Peng–Robinson 1978 kernel; a package is a method plus a compound list, and the fluid network, the simulator and a built column each run on views of the network package `icheme:refinery` restricted to the compounds they hold. The column takes only views without crystals or a reference spine. Package data are identified by declared revisions, never by content hashes. Both route all transcendental math through `DeterministicMath`, so results are bitwise identical on every JDK; the column's optional Rust library (off by default) gives the same bits ([column-v3-solver.md](column-v3-solver.md)).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    P[Player: place / edit / open GUI] --> Q[Server: queued engine event]
    Q --> S[Scheduler: due deadline or dependency change]
    S --> W[Bounded CPU workers: column solve / island step]
    W --> D[Server thread: drain completions]
    D --> B[Presentation bucket, about every 5 s]
    B --> C[Clients with an open GUI request]
```

Four standing rules shape this flow (AGENTS.md):

- The engine owns presentation. GUI data goes only to clients that requested it. Opening a GUI may replay the cached snapshot, and nothing else.
- No process calculation runs on the tick loop. The tick only checks what is due, drains completions and flushes a bucket.
- Saves keep facts. Fluid and equipment saves keep what the player built and put in (registrations, contents, ledger totals, shapes, paint) and rebuild what the engine can compute again; a damaged facts section refuses the world. Other formats (wire protocols, the column block) carry a version and get no compatibility work.
- No range holds. A state outside a data range continues, its value and slope continuous, with a status notice. Only numerical guards (the 10 to 6000 K, 1 Pa to 1 GPa box of `NumericalBox`) reject a trial step.

### Shortest reading paths

- **Column:** `V3ColumnInput` → `V3ColumnCalculator` → `V3ColumnProblem` → residual evaluator → simultaneous solver → auditor.
- **Fluid:** `PhysicalRegistry` → `PhysicalFluidTopology.compile` → `IslandCoordinator` → `PassiveIntervalSolver` → `PassiveStepSolver` (`BalanceStart`, `StepEquations`) → `ConservativeTransport` → `FluidPresentation`.
- **Equilibrium:** `EquilibriumRequest` → `FluidTpEquilibrium.tp/ph/uv` (the flashes, the crystal set and the critical band in files beside it) → `PhaseContract` checks → `TangentPlaneStability` → flash (up to three fluid phases; water a compound of the cubic) / crystals → `EquilibriumResult`; the network's entry is `NetworkPhaseEngine`.
- **Equipment:** `EquipmentShape` → `ShapeRasterizer.layout`/`key`/`solid` → `EquipmentStructure.place` → `EquipmentShellBlockEntity` → `EquipmentShellModel` (client); a unit's parts: `UnitDesign.of(kind)` → its `*Design.build` → `ProcessUnit` kit.
- **Line laying:** `LineToolSession` → `LineLaying.search` → `LineAnchors`/`LinePlacement`/`LineLayout` → `LineNetwork` → `LineLaying.execute` → shared admission/commit → `FluidLineBackend` or `ConduitLineBackend`. `LinePieces` owns derived cross-cell lead occupancy and `LineToolMarkers` the shared marker cache.
- **Assembly:** `EquipmentShellBlock.use` → `AssemblyNetwork.open` → `AssemblyPresentation` (open, validate: queued) → `AssemblyService.tick` → `validate` → `AssemblyWalker` and `AssemblyMatcher` (on `ColumnBlueprint.of`) → `AssemblyStore`; the reverts: `FluidWorldAuthority.listen` and `EquipmentStructure` → `AssemblyService.edited`/`placed`/`removed`.
- **Heat:** `HeatSides.of` → `EquipmentStructure` (`placeUnit`) → `PhysicalFluidTopology.compile` (a `HEAT_SIDE` as one pipe with its `HeatLaw`) → `IslandCoordinator` and `ExchangerCoupling` (the fence and the reads) → `PassiveStepSolver.plan`/`couple` (`ExchangerRelay`, `HeatControl`) → `ConservativeTransport` (HEAT booking) → `HeatPresentation`.
- **Running column:** `AssemblyService.start` → `ColumnStarts.build` → `FluidWorldAuthority.startColumn` → `ColumnOperation.refresh` → `ColumnSolve.run` (`ColumnSolve.calculate`, cold start `CLASSICAL`) → `SplitTable.of` → `ColumnOperation.cycle` (`ColumnCycle.step`) → `IslandCoordinator` (`plans`, `booked`) → `PassiveStepSolver.ports`.
- **Simulator:** `ProcessSimulatorScreen` (Run) → `SimulatorRunner` (its thread) → `FlowsheetEngine.run` → `FlowsheetCompiler.compile` → `FlowGraph.plan` → `FlowsheetSolver.run` (`ColumnUnit` → `ColumnBlockSolve` → `ColumnSolve.calculate`, cold start `LEARNED`) → `SheetSizing.rate` → `ProcessSimulatorNetwork` (upload to `SheetStore`).
- **Machines:** `ShaftParts` (queued `ShaftTrains.Event`) → `ShaftTrains.apply` → `ShaftNetwork.compile` → `ShaftTrains.plans` → `ShaftPlan.apply` (dispatch) → `PassiveStepSolver` (`MOVER_LAW`, `FlowControl.Machine`) → `ShaftTrains.booked` → `ShaftTrains.decide` → `ShaftTrain.step` → `ElectricStores.draw` / `MachinePresentation`.
- **Data:** `src/main/resources/data/icheme/materials/` (with `methods/` and `crystals/`; `spine/` and `group_interactions/` only in the pilot test overlay) → `MaterialReloadListener` → `MaterialCatalog` (`restrict` for a view) → `MaterialRuntime`.
