# Industrial Chemical Engineering (IChemE) code guide

**Wired control and power:** local boxes run ideal loops; DCS monitors and changes settings over data cable. Pipes, data and power share laying/rack adapters; standard FE serves motors/fans/inline movers and a six-sided creative RF source. Details: [wired-control-power.md](wired-control-power.md).

**Rotating machinery:** pumps and compressors on their curves or as positive-displacement machines, cavitation on an NPSH3 floor, gas and wet suctions, vacuum pumps (liquid ring, dry screw), separate drivers (motor, steam turbine, combustion engine, gas turbine, creative motor, Create coupling) joined by shaft blocks and gearboxes into trains, FE stores for motors, generators and fans, pump wear and repair, machine pages and sounds. Details: [rotating-machinery.md](rotating-machinery.md).

Details: [process-simulator.md](process-simulator.md).

**Water cooling towers:** Induced-draft banks of parallel fan cells and hollow natural-draft towers, with a Merkel rating fill, real open basins and conserved exchange with the air. Details: [cooling-towers.md](cooling-towers.md).

## Level 1 — Overview

Industrial Chemical Engineering (IChemE, formerly CreateChemE) is a Java 21 addon for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge 21.1.219; Create 6.0.10 and KubeJS optional since batch 2026-11-03-rotating-machinery). It simulates chemical-process equipment with real thermodynamics. It has two process systems and an equipment layer:

- **Distillation column (V3).** The player enters a crude feed, a tray count, pressure, steam, side draws, pumparounds, a condenser and a reboiler. The column calculator then solves the coupled material, equilibrium and energy balance (MESH) equations with a damped Newton method on a log-coordinate state. A learned (Transformer) seed comes first; the classical initializer is the fallback. A result is published only after the convergence and physical-acceptance audits pass. Since batch 2026-11-02-process-simulator no block runs it on its own: the process simulator solves it on the client as one of its units, and a built column solves it on the server.
- **Fluid network.** Pipes, tanks, junctions, pumps, compressors, filters and phase ports are placed in the world; the pipe laying tool lays a whole line along a searched route, on pipe racks where they stand, and a laid pipe joins only through the faces its route uses. A pipe can carry a fitting (shutoff, check or control valve, Y-strainer, inline pump or compressor, sight glass) and pressure or temperature gauges; process sensors (pressure, temperature, flow, level) report the accepted interval in the fluid screen, and a control valve with a pressure or flow module regulates inside the solve; a floating-roof tank is open to the atmosphere, so it vents its gas, overflows at its fill height and spills over its shell. Heat units exchange heat between streams: an exchanger's two sides through Standard or Low-dP crossflow routing, an air cooler against ambient air, a fired heater's radiant and convection coils, a kettle's tubes boiling the pool in its shell; each side holds its outlet by its own bypass, fans or burners, or works a real valve once its lines check complete. They form *islands*: connected sets that each own one solver. Each island advances in simulated online time (20 TPS nominal) with an implicit step: pressure-driven flow through pipe resistance (the 100 m/s velocity cap is part of each throttling line's law), phase layout in vessels, transport of solids and slurry, and a multiphase equilibrium engine. The engine runs tangent-plane stability with TP, PH and UV flashes on the PR78 kernel (E-PPR78 kij(T) for group packages), every phase at its own pressure, IF97 Region 1 water with its steam a second-virial gas (IF97 Region 2's B), and crystals (CO2-I, ice Ih) that form and melt in vessels, move as solids and melt on arrival in a junction above their melting point. A solve without a carried point starts from the island's quasi-steady balance. Leaving a data range never holds an island; the status line notes it.
- **Process simulator.** A block with a drag-and-drop flowsheet: feeds, units and streams, a sequential-modular run with torn loops on the client (never on the server), the V3 column as one unit, sizing of every unit and line against Must and Should rows with Auto/Manual equipment choices and a 20 % spare, and Send to block, which hands sized designs to the equipment constructor. The block only stores the sheet and results and lets one player edit. See [process-simulator.md](process-simulator.md).
- **Equipment.** An equipment constructor builds and deconstructs equipment; direct breaking requires Creative and other ordinary removal paths are blocked. It builds drums, distillation columns and 26 kinds of process units (among them pump and compressor casings, a steam turbine, motor, generator and gearbox, each with its design point) whole on the block grid, drawn per cell in the chunk mesh with 1/8-block collision; drums, floating-roof tanks and spheres are fluid-network reservoirs with their design volume, level height and nozzle ports. Fully constructed pumps and both compressor models register one directed fluid device, a steam turbine one too; exchangers, kettles, air coolers and fired heaters register one heat side per passage (a kettle's shell a vessel); other columns/units remain drawing-only. A column is built only from a design the process simulator saved; with the units, valves and lines round it, it is an assembly that "Check parts" checks against that design and that reverts to not validated when a part it relied on changes. An exchanger whose control asks for a bypass or a ratio station is an assembly too. See [equipment.md](equipment.md), [heat-exchangers.md](heat-exchangers.md) and [machine-assemblies.md](machine-assemblies.md).
- **Rotating machinery.** A constructed pump or compressor runs on its own law: a centrifugal curve at the speed its driver sets, or a positive-displacement line with slip and a relief valve; a pump cavitates on an NPSH3 floor and takes gas, a compressor takes liquid. Shaft blocks and gearboxes join casings to one driver each (a motor on FE, a steam turbine with internal admission regulation, a liquid-fuel combustion engine or a gas turbine on its fuel line, a creative motor, or with Create a Create coupling). Drivers step once per slice from the power their machines booked; motors, generators and fans keep electric stores; pumps wear from cavitation and running dry and are repaired with a kit. See [rotating-machinery.md](rotating-machinery.md).

Both systems share a material catalog loaded from the datapack (components, datasets, methods, packages, presets) and a Peng–Robinson 1978 kernel; a package is a method plus a compound list, and the fluid network, the simulator and a built column each run on views of the network package `icheme:refinery` restricted to the compounds they hold. The column takes only views without crystals or a reference spine. Package data are identified by declared revisions, never by content hashes. Both route all transcendental math through `DeterministicMath`, so results are bitwise identical on every JDK. The one exception is the column's C++ outer pass, on by default where the jar carries the library ([column-v3.md](column-v3.md)).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    P[Player: place / edit / open GUI] --> Q[Server: queued engine event]
    Q --> S[Scheduler: due deadline or dependency change]
    S --> W[Bounded CPU workers: column solve / island step]
    W --> D[Server thread: drain completions]
    D --> B[Presentation bucket, about every 5 s]
    B --> C[Clients with an open GUI request]
```

Four standing rules shape this flow (AGENTS.md):

- The engine owns presentation. GUI data goes only to clients that requested it. Opening a GUI may replay the cached snapshot, and nothing else.
- No process calculation runs on the tick loop. The tick only checks what is due, drains completions and flushes a bucket.
- Saves keep facts. Fluid and equipment saves keep what the player built and put in (registrations, contents, ledger totals, shapes, paint) and rebuild what the engine can compute again; a damaged facts section refuses the world. Other formats (wire protocols, the column block) carry a version and get no compatibility work.
- No range holds. A state outside a data range continues, its value and slope continuous, with a status notice. Only numerical guards (the 10 to 6000 K, 1 Pa to 1 GPa box of `NumericalBox`) reject a trial step.

## Misleading names (details in the chapters)

- `PassiveNetwork` also holds pumps and compressors. `Reservoir` means any node. A junction's `Inventory.internalEnergy` holds `m·h`.
- `FluidThermodynamics.thermodynamicRevision()` still starts `fluid-trbdf2-r1`, but the integrator is backward Euler. Checkpoints save the string in each package record and compare it at load.
- `FlowControl.Mode` ordinals (9 constants) are saved in checkpoints: the island unit's endpoint modes. Do not reorder or remove a constant without a format bump (format 11 removed the three velocity-limit modes; unit format 4 states the eight ordinals left; the process-control batch appended `VALVE_CONFLICT`, ordinal 8).

- `columnDensity` and "static column" in the fluid code (`PassiveStepSolver`, `NetworkReduction`) mean the fluid standing in a line or port (decision D34), not the distillation column.
