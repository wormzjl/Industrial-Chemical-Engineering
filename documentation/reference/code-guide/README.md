## Level 1 — Overview

Industrial Chemical Engineering (IChemE, formerly CreateChemE) is a Java 21 addon for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge 21.1.219, Create 6.0.10). It simulates chemical-process equipment with real thermodynamics. It has two process systems and an equipment layer:

- **Distillation column (V3).** The player enters a crude feed, a tray count, pressure, steam, side draws, pumparounds, a condenser and a reboiler. The column calculator then solves the coupled material, equilibrium and energy balance (MESH) equations with a damped Newton method on a log-coordinate state. A learned (Transformer) seed comes first; the classical initializer is the fallback. A result is published only after the convergence and physical-acceptance audits pass. The calculator also saves a column design (trays, diameter, components) that the equipment constructor imports.
- **Fluid network.** Pipes, tanks, junctions, pumps, compressors, filters and phase ports are placed in the world; the pipe laying tool lays a whole line along a searched route, on pipe racks where they stand, and a laid pipe joins only through the faces its route uses. A pipe can carry a fitting (shutoff, check or control valve, Y-strainer, inline pump or compressor, sight glass) and pressure or temperature gauges; process sensors (pressure, temperature, flow, level) report the accepted interval in the fluid screen, and a control valve with a pressure or flow module regulates inside the solve; a floating-roof tank is open to the atmosphere, so it vents its gas, overflows at its fill height and spills over its shell. Heat units exchange heat between streams: an exchanger's two sides through a counterflow wall, an air cooler against ambient air, a fired heater's radiant and convection coils, a kettle's tubes boiling the pool in its shell; each side holds its outlet by its own bypass, fans or burners, or works a real valve once its lines check complete. They form *islands*: connected sets that each own one solver. Each island advances in simulated online time (20 TPS nominal) with an implicit step: pressure-driven flow through pipe resistance (the 100 m/s velocity cap is part of each throttling line's law), phase layout in vessels, transport of solids and slurry, and a multiphase equilibrium engine. The engine runs tangent-plane stability with TP, PH and UV flashes on the PR78 kernel (E-PPR78 kij(T) for group packages), every phase at its own pressure, IF97 Region 1 water with its steam a second-virial gas (IF97 Region 2's B), and crystals (CO2-I, ice Ih) that form and melt in vessels, move as solids and melt on arrival in a junction above their melting point. A solve without a carried point starts from the island's quasi-steady balance. Leaving a data range never holds an island; the status line notes it.
- **Equipment.** An equipment constructor builds drums, distillation columns and 22 kinds of process units whole on the block grid, drawn per cell in the chunk mesh with 1/8-block collision; drums, floating-roof tanks and spheres are fluid-network reservoirs with their design volume, level height and nozzle ports. Fully constructed pumps and both compressor models register one directed fluid device; exchangers, kettles, air coolers and fired heaters register one heat side per passage (a kettle's shell a vessel); other columns/units remain drawing-only. A column is built only from a design its calculator saved; with the units, valves and lines round it, it is an assembly that "Check parts" checks against that design and that reverts to not validated when a part it relied on changes. An exchanger whose control asks for a bypass or a ratio station is an assembly too. See [equipment.md](equipment.md), [heat-exchangers.md](heat-exchangers.md) and [machine-assemblies.md](machine-assemblies.md).

Both systems share a material catalog loaded from the datapack (components, property packages, presets) and a Peng–Robinson 1978 kernel; the column takes only packages without crystals or a reference spine. Package data are identified by declared revisions, never by content hashes. Both route all transcendental math through `DeterministicMath`, so results are bitwise identical on every JDK. The one exception is the column's C++ outer pass, on by default where the jar carries the library ([column-v3.md](column-v3.md)).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    P[Player: place / edit / open GUI] --> Q[Server: queued engine event]
    Q --> S[Scheduler: due deadline or dependency change]
    S --> W[Bounded CPU workers: column solve / island step]
    W --> D[Server thread: drain completions]
    D --> B[Presentation bucket, about every 5 s]
    B --> C[Clients with an open GUI request]
```

Four standing rules shape this flow (AGENTS.md):

- The engine owns presentation. GUI data goes only to clients that requested it. Opening a GUI may replay the cached snapshot, and nothing else.
- No process calculation runs on the tick loop. The tick only checks what is due, drains completions and flushes a bucket.
- Saves keep facts. Fluid and equipment saves keep what the player built and put in (registrations, contents, ledger totals, shapes, paint) and rebuild what the engine can compute again; a damaged facts section refuses the world. Other formats (wire protocols, the column block) carry a version and get no compatibility work.
- No range holds. A state outside a data range continues, its value and slope continuous, with a status notice. Only numerical guards (the 10 to 6000 K, 1 Pa to 1 GPa box of `NumericalBox`) reject a trial step.

## Facts the old guide got wrong

The 2026-09-08 guide is out of date on these points:

- V1 `ColumnSimulation` is gone, and so is the V3 preconditioner framework (production and test tree). The one bubble-point projection is `V3ColumnCalculator.projectedSeedOrPrevious`.
- The column keeps no retained result: `tryBegin` clears the display, and a loaded result shows as STALE. The default route is `LNN_FIRST` (bundled model `regrouped-seed-17041-epoch-160`). There is no learned recovery stage (`RAMP_HANDOFF` is gone) and one correction rule (`Correction.PROGRESS`).
