1. Expected (physics). A mover's shaft gives the fluid a work W over a slice; the solve books it (`PassiveStepSolver.moverPower`, 1929 to 1932, at the suction's own heat-capacity ratio `kappa(graph, pipe)` and wet efficiency; `ConservativeTransport` books it, 594 to 604). The driver must be charged that same W (plus its own losses: the motor's efficiency, the in-line drive's), so that electricity in equals work to the fluid plus stated losses.
2. What the code does. The train and the stores compute the power a second time with `k = 1.4` and no wet factor: `ShaftTrains` 672 (`m.law().shaftPower(q, m.speed(), density, suction.pressure(), 1.4)`), 683 (`c.power(..., 1.4)`), `ElectricStores` 499 (the same, and for an in-line set-flow compressor `mover.power(...) / .65` over a power that already holds its efficiency), `InlineMachines.ratedShaftPower` 50 to 53, `ColumnPower` 45. Charged against the work the gas gets: +13 % for a propane frame at ratio 3 (k 1.13), +4 % on steam or methane, -2.6 % to -11 % for a wet centrifugal machine, +54 % for an in-line set-flow compressor (MACHINES_REVIEW M-01; check each number against the formulas).
3. The fix. The turbine case already does it right (`ShaftTrains` about 693 to 697): it reads the slice's booked `BoundaryTransfer` of kind `SHAFT` for its pipe and divides by `result.advancedSeconds()`. First find in `ConservativeTransport` whether a pump's and a compressor's work is booked as such a transfer too (same kind, the mover's node id). If it is: the `Machine` and `Compressor` cases of `ShaftTrains.booked` and `ElectricStores.booked` read that booked energy (sign as the fluid receives it) and no law is evaluated a second time. If it is not: add to `PassiveIntervalSolver.Result` one array, the shaft work each connection gave over the slice (J, the sum the reconstruction booked, in edge order), filled where the work is booked, and read that. Either way the number is the booked one, not a recomputation, so the energy ledger and the driver agree exactly. `ColumnPower` (a held casing booked by its column) and `InlineMachines.ratedShaftPower` (a rating shown before any slice exists) have no slice to read: they take the suction's `kappa` and the wet factor through the same function the solve uses (make it reachable; do not copy it), and the rating says in its comment that it is a design figure.
4. M-07. `SpeedControl.requiredSpeed` at `ShaftTrains` 673 to 675 and 686 to 689 inverts the curve with 1.4. Pass the suction's heat-capacity ratio the solve used: the result carries it per mover connection (one array beside the work), or the function of item 3 gives it from the suction state.
