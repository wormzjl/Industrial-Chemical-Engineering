## Findings (from the code, before the change)
1. **Where `SparseLuSolver.Storage` is built on the engine's path.** The premise of PROFILE_REVIEW "After wave B" item 3 does not hold for the two storages it names:
   - `ConservativeTransport.Workspace` is a final field of `PassiveStepSolver`, which `PassiveIntervalSolver` holds and the island's `RetainedSolver` keeps across jobs. Built once per handle.
   - The step Newton's `SparseNewton.Workspace.Shared`: one per active-set structure in `PassiveStepSolver.structures` (four kept). The dt-keyed workspaces fork from it and share it; `replayStart` forks it too. A new structure (phases, support, modes, closed boundaries, junction donors) builds a new one.
   - **The per-step storages are the solves that build their own workspace:** `SparseNewton.solve(equations, initial, settings, checkpoint)` built `new Workspace()`, so a new storage, a new `UpLookingLu` whose first `prepare` allocates L and U at 4 nnz + n (`UpLookingLu` 198, 199: the profile's lines), and a search of every column. Its callers on the step's path: a vessel's closure (`InventoryEquilibrium.close` 246, from `ConservativeTransport.reconstruct` 663 for every solved vessel whose booked inventory misses 1e-11, again after a fold, and from a phase check's vessel split), a junction's split (`FluidThermodynamics.junctionSplit` 1110, from `PassiveStepSolver` 1629, 2911 and `phaseCheck` 1001), a crystal's joint closure (`VesselCrystals` 312). So about one storage per vessel per reconstruction (one reconstruction per converged pass), plus one per split. Rare ones: the gate's polish (`PassiveStepSolver` 832), the negative-component re-solve (`ConservativeTransport` 440), a junction mint, and a solids island's `SolidEventIntegrator` (a new step solver per job).
   - Not measured here: `luStorages` per accepted interval on `mixed100` (with `-PfluidBenchmarkDiagnostics=true`) is the reading that confirms it.
2. **Which matrices one storage factors.** A Newton family: the Jacobian of one structure (its numeric pattern varies only where a derivative is exactly zero). The transport storage: the fluid system, an island with a filter's solid system, each melting group's matrix, and the rate solves' port-graph reconstructions, all through one `UpLookingLu`. Where an island has two of these, the patterns alternate and every factorization searches every column.
3. **What the island retains.** `RetainedSolver` (latch `SolverOwnership.released()`, acquired per job by the worker, released in a `finally`) holds the interval solver, the step solver, its structure workspaces, orderings and the transport workspace. `replayStart` drops every Jacobian and LU at a job's start and keeps pattern, colouring and ordering. A new handle (all storages rebuilt) follows every hold, a certificate (dropped while certified), a certificate wake or revalidation, a property suspension, and a topology change (a new island).

## Item 1 (the gain): the LU storages stay with the island
- **Closure storages kept** (`SparseNewton.KeptFactors`): an island's cache of storages by declared Jacobian pattern (eight kept, least recently used out). `PassiveIntervalSolver` holds one, with the island's latch, and binds it to the worker thread for each interval solve (`around`). The 4-argument `SparseNewton.solve` then borrows the pattern's storage at its first factorization and gives it back in a `finally`. Each solve still builds its own workspace: pattern, colouring, ordering, Jacobian and derivative buffers are new, as before. A borrowed storage is out of the cache, so a nested solve of one pattern gets another. Unbound, or where the thread does not hold the latch (`SolverOwnership.heldByCurrentThread`), a solve builds its own storage as before.
- **Transport storages by structure**: `ConservativeTransport.Workspace` keeps an ordering and a storage per structure (four kept), so its patterns no longer alternate through one LU.
- **The step Newton is unchanged.** Its storage stays with its fork family: a refactorization supersedes the family's older factors, which the chord relies on.
- **Bit for bit:** `UpLookingLu`'s class comment, "Reuse across factorizations". Nothing numeric carries from one factorization to the next. `pinv` is reset. The dense column is cleared on each reach before use, and only reach rows are read. The marks are cleared after each search. L and U are written from 0. Only capacity and the kept reach, a function of the pattern and the pivots, carry over. The storage's scaling, permutation, solve and check buffers are written before they are read. Each factorization is consumed before the next one in the same storage. RAM: per kept storage about 24 (4 nnz + n) bytes of L and U plus O(n); at most 8 closure patterns and 4 transport structures an awake island.
- **Counters** (`SolverDiagnostics`, behind its switch; the `UpLookingLu` statics are gone): `luStorages`, `luColumnsFactored`, `luReachSearches`. They print in `FluidServerBenchmark`'s `report.json` (diagnostics, counters and per accepted interval) with `-PfluidBenchmarkDiagnostics=true`, and in the chain-100 table. Expected on `mixed100`: `luStorages` per interval near 0 once the islands are warm; `luReachSearches / luColumnsFactored` near 0; `UpLookingLu.prepare` gone from the allocation profile.
- **Test** `runtime.fluid.KeptLuStorageTest`: nitrogen from a generator through two tanks (two vessels with one closure pattern), two warm-up jobs, then three one-step jobs on the kept handle against a fresh handle each.
  - Asserted: the same bits (flows, inventories, pressures, temperatures); the same counts of columns, factorizations, Jacobians and Newton solves; on the kept handle 0 storages and 0 reach searches.
  - If only the last assertion fails, a pivot changed between jobs, which is a search the kept reach cannot save, not a defect: read the printed counters.
- **Kernel workspace (F0's item, not done):**
  - The closures build a new `PhaseLayout`, so a new `Prepared` and kernel workspace each call (`junctionSplit` 1107; `InventoryEquilibrium.layout`).
  - The same thread-bound island cache can hold a `Prepared` per closure role and support, refilled by `FluidThermodynamics.prepare(T, reuse)` (B-S1's P-18 refill, bit-equal). The decode bundles that the accepted pass drops (E2) could stay on the step solver per node.
  - Cost: an API in `PhaseLayout` and `FluidThermodynamics`, and about two kernel workspaces per node per island in RAM (P-10's choice).
