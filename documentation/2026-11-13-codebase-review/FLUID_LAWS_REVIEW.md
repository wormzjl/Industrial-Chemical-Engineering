## 1. Summary

The area holds the network's device laws (pipe friction, movers, valves, heat laws, the cooling tower, the open and blanketed vessels, phase-port draw, crystals), the exact booking after each step (`ConservativeTransport`, `InventoryEquilibrium`), the interval controller (`PassiveIntervalSolver`, `SolidEventIntegrator`), transport properties (viscosity, slurry, mobility), the solid stock record, the fluid conversion rules and the diagnostics counters. Most laws are pure functions with no unknowns of their own, planned per step or per slice: a good shape for a flat port.

Five largest problems:
1. The cooling tower runs a stack of nested root searches inside every Newton residual (`TowerTransfer` 18, 482-510; called from `PassiveStepSolver` 5368). These are the air flow, the fall, the wet bulb and, in TARGET_TEMPERATURE mode, the fan speed. Each residual also makes 4 EOS liquid calls (`TowerTransfer.of` 290-293). The fan-speed controller in the residual breaks the stated controller rule (`HeatControl` 8: "never inside a Newton").
2. `ConservativeTransport` (869 lines, 11 maps) builds its matrix as `List<TreeMap<Integer,Double>>` and copies it up to 3 times (355, 368-369, 428). `pinnedFlows` and `reach` cost O(nodes x edges) (788-799, 704-708). One fresh LU is built per negative component (440).
3. Per step, `ControlConstraints` eliminates junction balances with a dense E x E basis three times (`standing`, `series`, `moverBounds`). The result depends only on the graph structure (`PassiveStepSolver` 379-381, 1873).
4. Design and geometry sit in the law package: `Chimney.Form` and its sizing, the `Flare` tip range and stack height, the `CoolingTower` cell geometry, `VesselGeometry`. Lower layers import upward: `transport` → `network` (`SlurryTransport` 3, `SolidMobility` 3/38), `machine` → `fluid/network` (`InlineMachines` 3), and the cycle `material` ↔ `fluid/state`.
5. The same physics is written several times. There are three Illinois root finders (`ExchangerRelay` 209, `HeatControl` 61, `TowerTransfer` 335). The series cell march appears four times. Combustion stoichiometry appears twice (`Burners` 41-42, `Flare` 86-87). The gas constant has two values (8.31446261815324 and 8.314462618), and the air density three (1.185, 1.18, 1/1.185). The process simulator rates the STANDARD exchanger on a zone LMTD (`ExchangerRating.rate` 76) and the other types on the world's cell wall (`CrossflowHeat`).

Ten highest-value changes, by gain over cost:
1. F1: plan the tower's fan speed once per step (as `AirCooled.planned` does). Optionally freeze its air flow too. PHYSICS, small.
2. F2: cache the tower calibration per model, not in one static slot that islands on different compound lists overwrite in turn. PERF, results identical.
3. F3: cache `ControlConstraints` results per graph structure. PERF, results identical.
4. F4: give `ConservativeTransport` a flat CSC assembly and a topological sweep for acyclic flows. PERF, last bits.
5. F5: add one node→edge adjacency (CSR) on `PassiveNetwork`, shared by 8 scanning sites. PERF, results identical.
6. F6: replace the 56-step bisection in `VesselGeometry.heightAt` with a safeguarded Newton. `portHead` calls it twice per residual for every non-prism vessel. PERF, below 1e-15 m.
7. F7: delete `ScheduledTransfer`. Only tests create it; 52 call sites read it. STRUCTURE.
8. F8: move the design and geometry out of the laws and cut the four wrong-way imports. STRUCTURE.
9. F9: keep one root finder and one series march. STRUCTURE, results identical.
10. F10: cache the numeric value in `ParticleSize` and the moments in `SolidInventory`. PERF, results identical.

ConservativeTransport (brief item 4):
- What it books: the pinned flows (D46), then for each solved node and conserved quantity (C components and P populations) the mass fractions from one upwind M-matrix `diag(m_end + dt·Out) - Σ dt·q`. It adds phase-draw streams as fixed donors, filter capture, open-vessel releases, tower losses, junction melting (D61), work, heat and SHAFT boundaries. It then restates each node (`repartition`) and closes each vessel (`InventoryEquilibrium.close`, its own Newton).
- Cost per step: about 3(N+E) boxed TreeMap entries; one or two LU factorizations with a backward-error check, plus one more per negative component (425-444); O(J·E) for `pinnedFlows`; O(P·N·E) for `reach`; and per vessel at least one thermo residual, often a Newton. The thermo dominates.
- Flat form: arrays `donor[E]`, `receiver[E]`, `rate[E]`, `drawn[E·K]` (phase-draw edges only) and the population index on an island table. The CSC pattern comes from a counting sort, cached per (structure, flow-sign pattern). For an acyclic flow, a topological substitution with no LU; for each strongly connected component (a recycle), a small LU. All right-hand sides go in one `double[K·N]` block.

## 4. Open questions for the main thread

1. F1: may the tower's target controller lag one step, and may its air flow freeze per step (option B)? Both move tower references.
2. F14: is a viscosity model swap (Wilke → Herning-Zipperer) within "simplification", or a second model to avoid?
3. F12: should the simulator size every exchanger on the world's cell model, so that "sized" means "what the world delivers"?
4. F7: approve deleting `ScheduledTransfer` (tests only)? It touches `PassiveNetwork`'s constructor (S3).
5. F17: can Pump and Compressor compile to `Machine` at graph build without changing saved names, given that IslandCertificate 387 writes control text?
6. GasBlanket 99 caps a closed vessel's vent at VENT_SPEED × footprint (the roof rule). Should the blanket vent instead be capped by its valve bore?
7. Should the cycle `material` ↔ `fluid/state` be broken by moving `SolidInventory` into `material` (S2) or into the thermo core?
