## 1. Summary

What the area does. V3 solves a steady crude column (condenser, up to 64 trays, reboiler; side draws, stripping steam with free water, prescribed stage heat) as one MESH system in log-flow and temperature unknowns. A damped Newton solves one frozen problem; a continuation ladder in `V3ColumnCalculator` (stage grids, pressure legs, feature ramps, support and wet-set refreshes) supplies the seeds; an audit recomputes the state before a result is published.

The five largest problems.

1. The published state depends on the machine. The C++ pass is on by default and calls the platform's `cbrt`, `acos`, `cos`, `log` (D1). The hydraulic correction runs under a wall clock (D2). The closure of a result depends on whether a seed existed (D3). The learned route's budget counts checkpoint call sites (D6).
2. There are two Jacobians. The analytic stage-block Jacobian is exact, but only a finite-difference Jacobian may certify a step, so every solve pays at least one: `12 C + 12` whole-column property passes (C = 20: 252) where the analytic form needs one pass (P1).
3. `V3ColumnCalculator` (3 637 lines) holds ten responsibilities and a ladder of about fourteen nested recovery layers, each with its own cap and no total budget. Control flow reads strings (failure codes, audit families, path labels, event prefixes) (S1, S2, S3).
4. The representation is one object per entity, rebuilt for every attempt: ledger records, hash sets and a bipartite matching (P3), hash maps and linear scans in the block assembler (P4), a new state and four to eight arrays per residual evaluation (P5). A flat port needs the opposite.
5. The audit runs on attempts that cannot be published (P2), two of its three residual checks have a limit of 1.0 where the Newton stop is 1e-8 (D4), and two checks are copies of the formulation they check (A2).

## 4. Open questions for the main thread

1. D1: may the C++ pass be switched off by default in this batch (one line, no reference moves on a machine without the library; on a machine with the library the published products move by the 7e-13 relative that REVIEW_PLAN finding 9 records)?
2. D3: which policy for AGGRESSIVE: 1e-3 for the warm pass and its recovery, or strict for the first solve too?
3. D6: does the learned budget stay in polls (the port then copies every checkpoint site and uses the count as a path fingerprint), or move to a physical work unit?
4. P1: is the analytic Jacobian adopted in Java before the port? It removes about 700 lines from the port's scope and is likely a larger gain than the language change; B1's share of time in residual and Jacobian assembly decides.
5. S4: per item, delete or move to `tools/`: the stage-trace cutoff, the closure fraction API, the two export hooks, the native probes.
6. L1 and L4: B1 should report, for the column scenarios, the Newton iterations of the first rung with and without the inside-out trial and whether any shipped preset enters the pressure ladder.
7. The ladder has no total budget; only the caller's deadline bounds a solve. Should a solve carry one work budget (the unit of question 3) in place of the per-layer caps that only exist to bound cost?
8. S2 (package) writes the column thermo port. The port plan assumes water is inside it and that the kernel's pair model has a flat form (S1, item 4). Both need to be confirmed before stage (b).
9. `V3ColumnInput.MAX_STAGE_COUNT` 64 and `V3ComponentBasis.MAX_COMPONENTS` 64 allow 8 580 unknowns and a band workspace of about 53 MB. Is that maximum a real requirement, or can the contract be 64 trays with the bundled packages' component counts?
