## 1. Summary

**What the area does.** `science/equipment` turns a saved `EquipmentShape` (a fact) into its cells, nozzles, collision boxes, drawn polygons and datasheet numbers. It does this through the kit `ProcessUnit`, one `UnitDesign` for each kind, and `ShapeRasterizer` for drums and columns. `world/**` places those cells in the world, binds them to fluid registrations, and refreshes or re-aligns them after a load.

**Five largest problems**
1. `ProcessUnit` (4 856 lines) is three things in one file: the drawing kit, the drawings of 11 kinds (about 2 500 lines), and the rating laws that the simulator's sizing reads (`pumpFlow`, `heaterDuty`, `tubeSurface`, ...). For those 11 kinds, the design's `build` method only calls into it.
2. `EquipmentStructure` (1 760 lines) dispatches on the kind in four parallel paths: `register`, `restoreFluidBinding`, `unregister` and `reregister`. Each is a chain of 6 to 9 kind branches. The class also builds machine laws (`machineControl`) and repeats the same lookups many times.
3. **DEFECT.** Shape fields 9 to 11 (`ExchangerType`, `TowerBank`, `BatchStillOptions`) and the column's exchanger types bypass `ShapeRepair`. They are read with a bare `valueOf` that has no alias, so one unknown name throws through readers that are documented as "never throws". The result is lost or unbound facts.
4. Facts are tangled up with the drawing and with solver types:
   - `EquipmentShape.nozzles()` and `frameBox()` build the whole unit drawing.
   - `ColumnOperatingPoint` (a saved fact) is typed on 6 V3 classes.
   - `UnitDesign` is the machine-rating and sound facade for runtime, flowsheet and client.
   This blocks the layer split of REVIEW_PLAN section 2.
5. Wasted computation:
   - Each designed unit is drawn 2 to 3 times for each geometry.
   - The unit cache is keyed by the heading, which no design reads.
   - There are 22 ad hoc caches that clear everything when full.

**Ten highest-value changes, ranked by gain over cost** (ids as in section 3)
1. E10: read shape fields 9 to 11 and the column exchanger types through `ShapeRepair`, with alias maps. Small change; fixes the defect.
2. E11: build each designed unit once for each geometry and keep its mesh. A few lines; drawing cost halved.
3. E12: key `ProcessUnit.BUILT` by the shape with heading NORTH. One line; up to 4 times fewer builds.
4. E3: delete the 13 size forwarders in `ProcessUnit`. Mechanical, about 130 call sites.
5. E1: move the 11 kind drawings out of `ProcessUnit` into their design files. About 2 500 lines moved.
6. E7 and E8: split `EquipmentShape`'s registration into one `Registration` for each kind family, declared once on the design. Delete the repeated lookups.
7. E4: one `Sizes` record per design in place of about 500 overrides that return constants. About 1 200 lines removed.
8. E2, E5 and E9: put the rating numbers in one `UnitRatings` class, and the shape-to-law step in one `MachineRatings` class. Then `UnitDesign` and the world classes stop building machine laws.
9. E13: one `BoundedCache` (an LRU bounded by weight) for the 22 caches.
10. E18: make `ColumnOperatingPoint` a plain fact. Its conversion to `V3ColumnInput` moves to `column/operation`. Prepares the Rust port and protects saved points.

## 4. Open questions for the main thread

1. E20: should the layout stamp (an FNV hash) become a declared revision per design? The switch refreshes every placed structure once.
2. E14 B and E21: may drums share the units' skirt and saddle, and may all kinds share one manway? Both are drawing changes and each refreshes those kinds once. Columns stay as they are.
3. E10: is STANDARD the right repair target for an unknown exchanger type, logged as a named repair?
4. E2 and E19: should `UnitRatings` (the datasheet numbers that sizing reads) live in `equipment.design` or in `flowsheet/sizing`? I propose `equipment.design`, because the shape facts depend on them.
5. E16: is a shift of at most 2 ulp in a drum's registered volume acceptable under the results rule?
6. E17: is a client-hook interface the wanted pattern for the about 20 common-to-client calls?
7. E7: should the order of registration families be fixed as one enum on `UnitDesign`, or as a method each design overrides?

**Where REVIEW_PLAN sections 1 and 2 do not match the code**
- Section 1, item 7 misses several imports from `science/equipment`: those into `science/machine`, into `fluid/topology` and into `fluid/solver`. It also misses the reverse imports `column/operation` → `equipment`, which together with `equipment` → `column/v3` make a cycle.
- The section 2 diagram puts "equipment geometry" above plant. But the plant code (`column/operation`) reads equipment facts (`ColumnOperatingPoint`, `ColumnTrays`, `ColumnComponents`, `HeatSides`). So `equipment` must split into design facts, below plant, and geometry, above it. That split needs E15 first, because today the facts record builds the drawing.
