## 1. Summary

The process simulator is a client-side sequential-modular solver (`FlowsheetSolver`, Wegstein tears) over tiles that call the world's laws through `FlowThermo` (the world's TP flash), then a sizing stage that rates constructor sizes against one table of limits (`Limit`). The control, power and instrument runtimes are server-thread services owned by `FluidWorldAuthority`: controllers decide on a 100-tick cadence from published views, FE moves every tick over the data bus's cable graph, and accessories live in their own facts store.

Five largest problems:
1. **The sheet's sizing restates world laws and the copies disagree.** An ejector sized on 5 bar steam gets 2.4 times the capacity the world gives it (P3-01); a compressor stage's discharge reads 37 K cooler than the world's machine runs (P3-02); the still's boil-over threshold is 70 % in one place and 75 % in another with the same citation (P3-04); the counterflow exchanger, the chimney friction and the ejector's load are second laws (P3-13, P3-15, P3-03).
2. **Identity and dispatch by strings.** Saved controller bindings point at MD5 UUIDs of built-in channel names made from enum names and nozzle order (P3-06, a fact that silently moves); tile types and port names are switched in 13+ places in science and client (P3-07).
3. **Three facts-reader styles** in the control and accessory stores, two "open before SavedData" readers, exact key-set checks that force a format bump per appended field (P3-21).
4. **The control/instrument/power runtimes are one tangled module** around `FluidWorldAuthority`, with cadence literals (`% 100` six times), structural results rebuilt every cadence and linear store scans (P3-17 to P3-20, P3-26).
5. **Wire boilerplate:** 15 protocol strings in 11 registrars, six count-guard helpers with two exception types, five identical watch payloads, facts NBT used as wire format (P3-22 to P3-24).

Ten highest-value changes, gain over cost:
1. P3-01 ejector sizing reads `SteamEjector.capacity` (one line; real defect at non-default motive).
2. P3-04 one still fill threshold (`BatchStill.FILL`), `PUKE` deleted.
3. P3-02 compressor discharge on the world's polytropic exponent and the model's k.
4. P3-06 declared, append-only built-in channel ids with aliases, pinned by a gate.
5. P3-03 + P3-09 the ejector law and its load into `science/machine`, both callers on it; inverts machine → flowsheet.
6. P3-10 + P3-11 one design-excess constant and one constants class (R, g, atmosphere).
7. P3-16 + P3-12 dedupe the level loop and the motor/pump-duty proposal.
8. P3-19 index the accessory store by host; stop dirtying it every 5 s.
9. P3-22 + P3-23 one protocol string and one wire helper.
10. P3-21 one facts reader and opener for control, accessory and assembly stores.
