## 1. The expected behaviour

The pot's heat Q boils the charge; the condenser duty, close to Q at steady boiling, goes to the cooling water. At a slice start *s* the only values certain on every machine are the ones committed through *s* − F (F = `FENCE_TICKS` 440, 22 s of play, 88 s of process at factor 4), because every island that writes them is waited for through that tick. So every value the water side, an outlet or the pot reads of another island must be dated at or before *s* − F. The result is a fixed lag, the same on every machine. At 20 kW the water takes the duty 88 s of process later and the heat in transit (a fact) holds about Q·F more: 1.76 MJ. Long-run energy is unchanged; the transit is an account.

## 2. What the code did

The water read the live transit at its dispatch, which holds every owe whose island's worker had already finished (0, 1 or 2 slices of duty: the review's scenario at ticks 1100/1110). Its rate read `r.heat` and its ceiling `r.solved` as the pot's latest dispatch left them. `condensed` read the same live values from a shared `paid` log that the pot's commit trimmed (F6). The outlets read `r.solved` and `r.condensed` live; the job's outlet floor read the water's record as of the pot's commit; the wakes fired when a commit or a completion landed. Only the outlets waited (for the pot); nothing waited for or on the water.

## 3. What was done (`Stills.java`, one commit `571264fe7`)

Each item: the values read, the tick each is dated with, and why each is booked on any schedule. "X through t" is `IslandCoordinator.through(X, t)`: committed through t, or held, parked, suspended, certified (materialised there) or gone.

1. **The heat due** (`due`). Read at the water's slice start *s*, fence f = *s* − F. Owes are dated by the end tick of the span that owed them (the outlet's gift, the pot's energy-only arrival) and kept in their own log, `Run.owes` (by tick, three slots: pot, distillate, vent). due = transit − Σ owes dated > f (strict: an owe dated f is booked, its island being through f). Booked: the water waits for the pot and both outlets through f, so every owe dated ≤ f is in the transit, and every owe dated > f that is in the transit is in the log (both are written in one call). The water's own bookings through *s* are certain (it is committed at *s*). One more step was needed for **bit** equality: (x + o) − o is not x, so the live form gives the same value to rounding only, which then reaches the water's solve. The read therefore carries due from its last read: due = due(last) + Σ owes in (f(last), f] in tick and slot order − the water's heat booked since that read (`since`, its own bookings). Where this differs from the live form by more than 1 J + 1e-6 of it (a load, a removed side, an owe the keep window dropped, a late owe of a held island) the live form stands. A retry of the same *s* reads the same value (nothing new in (f, f], `since` 0). F6: the owes log is trimmed by the water's reads (the owes dated ≤ f) and, for a water that stops reading, at an owe's tick − F − `KEEP_TICKS` (2400, the column's). The arrivals log (the outlets' reads) keeps F + `KEEP_TICKS` too, not 2F.
2. **The rate and the ceiling.** The pot's commit logs its slice's heat (`r.heat`, as its dispatch set it) and its dispatch temperature, dated by the slice's start (`Run.heats`). The water reads the latest entry dated before f (`heatAt`): the pot through f has committed every slice that starts before f. I log at the commit, not at the dispatch as the brief says: a held slice is dispatched again at the same start, and only the commit says which value counted; the value is the same `r.heat`. rate = heat(f) + max(0, due − hold·heat(f))/(6·hold). The ceiling takes the answer applying at or before f (`Run.answers`, written at the completion with its apply tick); the water also waits until no answer due by f is pending (`p.pending && p.applyTick <= f`), so every answer with apply ≤ f has arrived.
3. **The waits** (`ready`, `fenced`). Water: the pot's, the distillate's and the vent's islands through f, and the answer due by f. Outlet: the pot through f (as before) and the answer due by f. Pot: its water's island through its own *s* − F (new), and the answer due by *s* (as before). The island itself and a device in no island are skipped.
4. **`condensed`**, at the pot's commit of the slice from *s*: the water's reads dated before *s* − F (the pot waited for the water through it, so all of them are logged; a read at exactly the fence may not be), `Run.reads` (tick, due, inlet, the water's heat taken so far). It feeds the next job's reflux (the head accounts, facts) only through these inputs. The share is logged by the commit's end tick (`Run.shares`).

## 4. The waits cannot block each other

Let c_X be island X's committed tick (its next slice's start). X waits on Y only while c_Y < c_X − F. The still's edges: W→P, W→D, W→V, D→P, V→P, P→W, each with lag F. If a cycle X1 → … → Xk → X1 were all blocked, c_X2 < c_X1 − F, …, c_X1 < c_Xk − F; summing, c_X1 < c_X1 − kF: impossible. A cycle that mixes these edges with the coordinator's coupling fence (lag 0) or a column's waits (lag ≥ 0) has total lag ≥ F > 0, so the same sum excludes it. The answer waits depend on a worker completion, not on an island, and every still request completes (a failure is `StillHead.Failed`, also at shutdown). Water certified at rest: `through` materialises it to the tick (or to its horizon, then it solves); its owes' wake ends the certificate at the owe's tick + F, where it first reads them. Water held, parked or suspended (unloaded): through is true, the pot runs on, its share reads the last reads before the stop; the owes accumulate in the log and in the transit; past `KEEP_TICKS` of lag the log's cap drops the oldest and the next read takes the live form. Water absent (no island) or removed: no wait; no owe is logged without the water's registration.
