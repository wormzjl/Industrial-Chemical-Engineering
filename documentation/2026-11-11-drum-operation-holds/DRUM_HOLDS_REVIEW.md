## 5. Server crash on a restored certificate (fixed, `9c05850bd`)
- **Expected.** A certified island whose horizon passed before its deadline fired (a restored world, a fence released late) materialises to the horizon once, revalidates, and solves on. A column cycle that waited for it runs after.
- **What the code did.** The horizon's materialisation booked its replay; the booking ran the column's waiting cycle (`ColumnOperation.booked` -> `due()`), which read the same island through the cycle's tick (`portsThrough` -> `through` -> `materialise`). The inner call reached the horizon and ended the certificate; the outer one then read it (`certifiedStatus`, NullPointerException, server stopped).
- **Fix.** `IslandCoordinator.Ports` gets `decide()` at the engine's decision points (after the due deadlines, a pump, a round's close), as `Shafts` has; `Ports.booked` records only. `ColumnOperation.booked` notes that a cycle is due; `decide()` runs it. One place decides when a column materialises its islands.
- **Proof.** `ColumnRunTest.aHorizonPassedBeforeItsDeadlineFiresWithACycleDueBehindIt` (the condenser's water island certified, its horizon passed with no pass over due deadlines, the ports' islands held) failed before the fix with the server's stack, line for line (1352, 1275, 1256, 542, 525). After: `ColumnRunTest` 50, `ColumnProbeTest`, `ColumnSuspensionTest`, `VoidColumnTest`, `IslandCoordinatorTest` 18, `StillRuntimeTest` 7, `SteamEjectorRuntimeTest` 2 pass. The kept crash world was not run: the JUnit reproduces its stack.

## 2. "HELD: round deadline or worker failure" without the failure (fixed, `aac765065`)
- **Expected.** A held island says why: the worker's exception and message, or the round's deadline.
- **What the code did.** `MinecraftFluidRuntime` passed only the empty result; the coordinator wrote one fixed text for a round closed before its answer, an answer past the wall budget, a worker exception and a cancellation alike.
- **Fix.** `IslandCoordinator.completed(attempt, result, failure)`. The runtime names `worker failure: <Type>: <message>` (its stack to the log as a warning) or the terminal status (`worker cancelled`, `worker deadline exceeded`, `worker abandoned the solve`); a round closed before its answer, or answered past the wall budget, reads `HELD: round deadline`. The hold policy (`budgetHold`) is unchanged.
- **Proof.** `WorkerFailureStatusTest` (2 tests); with the old fixed text it fails. `IslandCoordinatorTest`, `ColumnRunTest`, `CarriedGasTest` pass.

## 3. Cold start under a drop leg (fixed, `e0a82728f`, narrowed in `9af97a3e6`)
- **Expected.** `DrumDrainTest` case 3: a 7.95 m3 drum holding nothing, vented to a void; 198.1 kg/s of water fed in; its outlet runs 2 m down to a flow valve at 592.8 kg/s, then 5 m to a 1 atm water void. Nothing flows through the leg's foot at the start, so it stands on its column to the void, 101.3 kPa less 5 m of water: about 52 kPa. The drum then fills and drains.
- **What the code did.** The mint seeds the foot junction as 1 L of the feed's water at 300 kPa: the supplier sweep passes through a vessel that holds no fluid. Its connections carry nothing from 300 Pa to 52 kPa (the drum's end is dry, a void gives nothing back), so every point there has a zero merit, and the balance's Newton accepted its first step at the hull's floor, 1e-3 of the highest pressure: 300 Pa, where the water is vapour (462 m3/kg). The step's Newton then stalled at residual 5.587 on the foot's row, the valve running -47.6 kg/s from the void: held in the first tick.
- **Fix (forced: the hull's bound is the search's box, not a balance).** `BalanceNet.rest`: after the balance, a junction the clamp left on the hull's bound with all its connections carrying nothing goes back toward its own pressure as far as they still carry nothing. Here 52.46 kPa, no flow. The sweep's sealed test and `rest` share one predicate (`still`).
- **First form, narrowed.** `e0a82728f` rested every no-flow junction. The quick check found cold seed 65 new (E5): two junctions of a cryogenic gas pocket at 83 to 84 kPa (114.9 K, 1.3e-4 liquid), inside a narrow no-flow band, were moved about 95 Pa to its edge, and the step stalled on their equilibrium rows (0.155). A junction inside a band is a balance anywhere in it; `9af97a3e6` leaves it.
- **Proof.** `DropLegColdStartTest` (case 3 in `src/test`): holds in its first tick at 5.587 with `rest` disabled; clean with it, foot 40 to 110 kPa, 60 s without a hold. `DrumDrainTest` case 3: clean over 1200 ticks, the drum cycling 0.8 to 3.5 % (option 1's regulated drain). Chain-100 exact (0 deviation). The 33 junction lines equal to option 1's (ms, bytes, allocated MB aside; `MIXED_GAS_COST` 313 Newton solves both). `PassiveStepSolverTest` and 7 other network classes, `StarvedRegulatingValveTest`, `SealedDischargeTest`, `InlineFilterLineHoldTest`, `PumpAheadOfFlowValveTest`, the junction transients; hold gates `JunctionSeedingGateTest` 7, `HoldClassWorldsTest` 41, `ExtremeTopologyIslandTest` 10 pass. Quick check 1-200 at factor 1 (`factor.sh dh2`), alone: cold 197 (85, 101, 118), bundled 199 (200), defaults 200, the batch's recorded run exactly.
- **Same cause as defect 1?** In part: both legs' foot junctions are minted as the feed's water through a drum holding nothing. Defect 3's hold is the balance's floor; defect 1's foot stood at 70.7 kPa, its column's own edge, which `rest` does not move. See GAS_VENT_REVIEW.md.
