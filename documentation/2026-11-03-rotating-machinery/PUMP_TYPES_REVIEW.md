# Pump types against refinery practice (review, 2026-11-04)

Batch `2026-11-03-rotating-machinery`, branch `claude/sad-elgamal-92e0e4` (W4). Owner: "review the pump types used in refinery, current large pump seems to be wrong type". Read-only review; no code changed. Water power below: 70 %, as `ProcessUnit.pumpPower`.

## 1 Expected behaviour

- **Casing rule.** API 610 (10th to 12th ed., clause "radially split casings") requires a radially split casing at a pumping temperature of 200 °C or more, for a flammable or hazardous liquid of relative density below 0.7, or for one above 100 bar rated discharge [A]. An axially split casing (BB1, BB3) is therefore for water, or for hydrocarbon that is cool, dense and below 100 bar.
- **OH2** (overhung, end suction, top discharge, centreline feet): the refinery's default process pump, used to its limit first because a BB pump costs 1.5 to 2 times more. Practice: up to about 300 to 500 kW; catalogues to 2,250 to 5,000 m³/h and 350 to 370 m [R §1]. Reflux, products, LPG (radially split by construction), small hot residue.
- **BB2** (between bearings, radially split, one or two stages, centreline mounted, usually top suction and top discharge): the large hydrocarbon process pump. Crude charge (400 kbpd = 2,650 m³/h on 2 to 3 BB2 in parallel, 1 to 3.5 MW), atmospheric and vacuum residue (340 to 400 °C), hot oil, coker charge, large pumparounds. Ranges: Goulds 3620 4,540 m³/h, 455 m, 455 °C; Flowserve HDX 4,100 m³/h; Sulzer BBS 5,000 m³/h, 450 m; KSB RPHb 550 m "top-top"; two-stage BB2 to 760 m [R §1, K].
- **BB1** (axially split, double suction, nozzles side by side below the split flange, foot or near-centreline mounted): cooling water, fire water, raw water, crude transfer and pipeline service at ambient temperature. 5,000 to 30,000 m³/h, 30 to 60 m typical in cooling water, to 215 to 300 m in catalogues [R §1]. Large cooling-water duties are also VS1 vertical pumps in the basin.
- **Multistage.** BB3 (axially split): moderate boiler feed and water injection. BB5 (barrel): high-pressure boiler feed, hydrocracker and hydrotreater charge at 150 to 200 bar, 1,000 to 2,000 m. VS6 (vertical double casing): low-NPSH services such as LPG and condensate loading. All three sit above the single-stage limit (BB2 450 to 550 m) or the 100 bar rule. The mod's 300 m cap (25 bar at relative density 0.85) is inside the single-stage OH2 and BB2 ranges, so none of them is needed. The exception is a low-flow, high-head duty (20 m³/h at 300 m: specific speed n√Q/H^0.75 ≈ 3), where real plants use multistage or high-speed OH6 pumps at a lower efficiency. At game level the single-stage drawing and 70 % stand in for it.
- **Size basis.** The mod's discharge is a line at 3 m/s; a real pump nozzle runs 6 to 9 m/s. The mod's largest pump, 0.6 m (3,054 m³/h), is a 14 to 16 in nozzle on a real BB2, inside the HDX, BBS and 3620 ranges. The 2026-10-17 finding "BB2 stops at 0.4 m" compared real nozzles with the mod's line sizes. By flow, BB2 covers the whole range the mod gives to BB1.

## 2 What the code does

- `ProcessUnit.pump()`: discharge d ≤ `OH2_MAX` = 0.4 m (≤ 1,357 m³/h; 528 kW at 100 m, 1.58 MW at 300 m) draws an OH2: axial suction at the front, vertical discharge on the casing centre line, centreline feet on pedestals. This matches practice, apart from the size of the largest high-head OH2 pumps (see option C).
- `splitPump()`: d of 0.45, 0.5, 0.55 and 0.6 m (1,718 to 3,054 m³/h; 0.67 to 1.19 MW at 100 m, to 3.6 MW at 300 m) draws a BB1. The casing is split at the shaft. Both nozzles are horizontal and opposite each other across the shaft, 0.5 m under it (suction on the constructor's right). The lower half stands on four feet on the baseplate. The choice depends on d only. The constructor does not know the service, so every large pump (crude charge, residue, hot oil, cooling water) is drawn as a cool-water split-case pump. At 200 °C and above, or in LPG service, API 610 forbids this pump.
- The drawing has no hydraulic effect: no size reaches the fluid network (plan §2). The choice changes looks and nozzle positions only.

## 3 Proposals (owner to choose)

- **A. Replace BB1 with a single-stage BB2 above 0.4 m.** It changes the 4 sizes from 0.45 to 0.6 m. The casing becomes a round radially split volute with a cover joint ring, centreline feet on pedestals (as OH2), and both nozzles vertical on top. The brackets, housings, shaft, coupling and baseplate stay.
  - Nozzles: every nozzle pipe block of these 4 sizes moves from the side faces to the top. This needs the owner's approval. Placed BB1 pumps rebuild with the new nozzles, and the pipes on their old side nozzles disconnect. `EquipmentLayoutGoldenTest` changes.
  - The pipe spacing rule (one free block between nozzle pipe blocks) puts the two top nozzles at least 2 m apart. With a 0.8 m suction, the casing or the necks must span this.
  - Code: deletes the split flange, studs, lugs, lower half, feet and side branches (about 40 lines). It reuses OH2's neck transition. The net line count stays about the same. The `.split` variant name and its five lang lines get new text.
- **B. BB2 for process service, BB1 kept, chosen in the constructor** (casing: process or water). This needs a new shape field, append-only, with the default BB1 above 0.4 m (today's behaviour). It also needs a corpus image, a constructor row and lang keys in five languages. No saved nozzle moves. B adds a second large drawing and a choice, against the owner's simplification rule. It matters only if cooling-water realism (cooling tower circuits) is wanted.
- **C (add-on to A or B). Switch from OH2 to BB2 also by power:** BB2 above about 600 kW (water, 70 %) at d ≤ 0.4 m. It changes only new design points: head above 114 m at 0.4 m, 148 m at 0.35 m, 202 m at 0.3 m and 291 m at 0.25 m. Pumps saved before W4 take 100 m (A3), at most 528 kW, so none of them moves. The threshold is refinery practice, not physics.
- **Not proposed:** two-stage BB2, BB3, BB5, VS6, VS1. The 300 m cap stays inside single-stage practice. Raise them only if the owner raises the head cap past about 450 m.
- **Recommendation: A.** In a refinery mod, the large pump is mostly hot or light hydrocarbon (crude charge, residue, pumparounds), where API 610 requires a radially split casing. A keeps one size switch and one large drawing. C is optional, for the owner to choose.
