# Implementation review

1. Expected behaviour: prefer a larger feasible unit to many small units, expose build count/dimensions and keep sizing mode separate from equipment configuration.
2. The old search preferred small surface candidates and the GUI mixed method with physical choices. Auto now ranks feasibility/spare, unit count and total surface, with Standard winning exact ties; Manual freezes the displayed type/count/dimensions and uses constructor-style controls. Dormant manual selections survive Auto and saves.
3. Owner-approved addition: Standard and Low-dP crossflow are sub-types of one exchanger family, not new equipment kinds or palette tiles. Dimensions and two tube passes retain their limits.
4. Crossflow uses 8 × 8 wall cells: shell flow mixes between transverse rows; tubes traverse two passes with return-header mixing. Existing phase films, heat curves, wall roots, bypass and transit conservation remain shared with rating and placed equipment. Standard and kettle pool calculations retain their own routing.
5. Crossflow shell geometry is explicitly a game approximation: free area 0.1 D L, path 2.5 D, hydraulic diameter 0.018 m, existing roughness. Tube-side pressure loss, both-side limits, 20% spare and unreachable-duty rejection remain active. Reduced shell loss is not a guarantee of a one-shell condenser.
6. Shared type/layout data feeds render geometry, occupied cells, nozzles, previews and assembly laying. Type carries through sent designs, imports, heat-unit facts and column members; assemblies identify a type mismatch. The later approved exterior places distribution spaces inside the shell rather than drawing rectangular headers outside it.
7. Tank gauge is an optional accessory. Compact tanks (D < 6 m and shell H < 6 m) omit internal roof access; a rim walkway requires D ≥ 6 m; a rolling ladder also requires H ≤ D. Tanks without that ladder use a fixed see-through safety grid and skip moving-roof player collision. Rim/landing access is aligned, with a straight external ladder and inner handrail.
8. Shared flange bolt/steel treatment, larger manway bands, compact top-in/top-out compressor without coolant, aligned guarded hex shafts, industrial constructor/simulator and persistent collision warning complete the model work.
9. Motors are VFD-controlled up to 3000 rpm with torque/power envelopes. A motor’s Auto demand follows exactly one data-linked pump/compressor on its shaft train. Other configurations require manual settings. Inline movers draw FE against line-size-based fixed ratings. The native Create adapter supports both directions; the legacy coupling remains.
10. The turbine’s internal admission governor regulates requested speed and available load. More available steam does not itself request overspeed; delivered work remains constrained by steam. New/unpowered machinery publishes zero rotation. Animation uses one nonlinear clock for rotating equipment and shafts; 1500/3000 rpm display 4.8/9 turns/s, asymptote 12 turns/s.
11. Local boxes own ideal control loops; DCS monitors and changes their settings over data cable. Stable human sensor IDs identify installed sensors. Structural installation/removal is immediate as the owner requested; measurements and loop updates remain on the engine’s 100-online-tick cadence. Flow/pressure/temperature reuse native laws; level inverts a volume balance over one cadence. No generic PID or per-tick process solve was added.
12. Pipes/data/power share anchors, route search, line selection, geometry, aim, validation and placement adapters, including rack lanes and existing stubs. Both conduits are 0.125 m metal housings; data is blue-banded and power orange-banded with a symbol about every four straight cells. Render refresh shares the normal model-data path.
13. Equipment data/power ports use casing/cabinet positions with clearance, centred where possible; motor sides are separate. FE uses the standard energy capability; creative RF serves all six faces. Graph caches rebuild on topology changes; no-source circuits skip sink probes.
14. Motors retain saved energy but use a 10 s rated buffer/5 s restart threshold and require live FE supply. Disconnect stops drive on the next scheduled process update even with stored energy. Other stores keep their existing capacities. This is scheduled shutdown, not immediate tick-level physics.
15. Facts are append-only: missing exchanger type means Standard; legacy poles/drive choices and stored joules remain readable. Compatibility corpus includes shape fields 9/10, fluid schemas 13/14, control formats 1–3, constructor designs and layout images. Damaged facts refuse load; disposable state rebuilds on mismatch.
16. Verification basis: CrossflowHeatTest checks analytic single-phase bounds, phase changes, reverse flow, bypass, conservation and finer grids; sizing/equipment tests cover constraints/imports/layouts; conduit/control/power and fresh-world GameTests cover integration. Final results and limits belong in MERGE_REVIEW.
17. Final consolidation: `LineToolSession` owns interaction; `LineLaying` owns all commands/previews; `LineLayout` merges both endpoints; `LinePlacement` owns admission/permissions/inventory; `LinePieces` owns cross-cell socket lead collision/selection. Fluid and conduit backends only own medium facts. There is no parallel cable command engine.
18. The DCS is two blocks tall with one lower-half identity/menu/socket. Existing one-block consoles wait for clear upper space without moving facts. All operator screens retain the player's GUI scale and use viewport scrolling/paging.
19. Constructor nozzle controls join its main door. Sensor hover shows coordinates and attached EQ ID, without a dimension. Motor/pump/generator sizing displays design RPM; retained pole fields are not exposed as design selectors.
20. `EquipmentServices` owns support-mounted cases and both socket datums. Rotating DATA is on the shared shaft-train side, FE opposite; small cases stay compact, larger DATA/FE cases match. Case/cover materials inherit equipment BODY paint. Native geometry feeds collision, constructor previews and drawings.
21. `EquipmentInstruments` owns manual drum/sphere/tank mounts and factory thermal/tower probes. Reinforced short metallic leads join facet-conforming textured H/V baselines; the sphere/vertical drum head arrangements and exchanger probe/nozzle sizes use those owners. Rear future-service pads remain clear. Both towers have factory level instruments.
22. `BuiltInInputs` exposes accepted internal channel IDs through one C1 and cached column assembly members. Bounded saved column probe state retains total-feed mass basis on reload; unavailable V3 circulating streams/air properties are not fabricated. No column energy/duty solver redesign was made.
