# Casing bolts and optional low polygon rendering

- Status: implemented in `238c136b` on `codex/simulator-sizing`, 2026-11-07; prepared for 0.22.0, not merged.
- Expected: normal machinery has raised casing fasteners; optional low detail reduces drawing cost while preserving the server's equipment and connections.
- Previously: pump/compressor cover nuts were baked into a face sprite in every mode; there was no drawing-detail setting.
- Config: `config/icheme-client.toml`, `[equipment] low_polygon_mode = false`. Default off. A changed mode reloads client render resources; F3+T also applies it.
- Multiplayer: client-only. Mixed normal/low clients use the same server. No setting enters packets, facts, shapes, layout stamps, collision, nozzles, shafts, sensors, fluid registrations or process calculations. No version/format identity changes are needed.

## Identified geometry and implementation

| Drawing | Low mode | Physical contract |
|---|---|---|
| Columns/drums, shell/head/skirt | Up to 24 angular sides, six head bands | Existing placement/collision and full-detail ports |
| Tanks/spheres, floating roof | Up to 24 sides; sphere up to 12 latitude bands | Physical roof radii, hinge/ladder frames and level unchanged |
| Exchanger/kettle barrels, round machinery, heaters/reactors | Shared round primitives capped at 24 sides/six head bands | Existing design dimensions, solids and interfaces |
| Natural cooling-tower shell/basin | Coarse shell and matching water perimeter | Basin volume, height, ports and duty unchanged |
| Pipe/nozzle/rack flanges, covers and fasteners | Flush textured hex heads; casing cover keeps its baked nut pattern | Small pipe profiles and route/placement engine unchanged |
| Shafts, rectangular frames, imported fan parts | Existing profiles retained | Hex shafts, access and animation contracts unchanged |

- Normal `PUMP_DISC` drawing becomes a plain `PUMP_COVER` with twelve raised nuts in a shallow recess. Bolt ends stay on the previous cover envelope, so footprints and ports do not expand. Low mode keeps the earlier textured cover. Non-fastener dark rods, valve stems and gaskets are not flattened.
- `RenderDetail` is an explicit immutable drawing argument. Normal geometry remains the canonical physics/layout cache; coarse geometry has a separate bounded cache and reuses canonical registrations/reach tables.
- Coarse facets can cross interior/empty cells. `EquipmentBodyRenderer` generalises the existing tower GPU renderer and `LoadedEquipment` generalises its reference-counted index. One complete cached mesh per structure avoids shell-cell clipping holes. No new per-cell ticker or solver work.
- Chunk models keep connection surfaces, markings and glass. Moving parts, floating roofs, constructor ghosts, basin water and sight-glass liquid follow the same active profile. Texture tiles are preserved. A published atlas/profile pair and resource reload prevent mixing old chunk models with a newly selected body mesh.
