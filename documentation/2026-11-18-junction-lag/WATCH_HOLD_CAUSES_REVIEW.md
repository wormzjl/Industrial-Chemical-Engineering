# Causes of the 13 rollback watch-world holds

At a committed boundary the worker must either advance the full requested interval with balanced mass, components and energy, or retain the last committed state. Filling or emptying a vessel, opening a boundary and changing phase can require smaller internal steps. A wall deadline records when the worker stopped. By itself it identifies neither the equation that failed nor whether the solver would have converged.

The rollback watch list held in 6 of 14 cold worlds and 7 of 13 bundled worlds, at time factor 4. A cold and a bundled world with the same number have different materials and graphs. The constructor merge at `d424a1a27` changed no production fluid solver sources. The rollback's separate 6,000-tick saved-plant run completed with zero warnings. [Rollback verification](JUNCTION_ROLLBACK_REVIEW.md)

## How to distinguish the remaining causes

1. The narrowest code correction is the fixed-node viscosity handoff. It changes a property lookup and can be tested independently of the reverted junction model.
2. At the filling transition in bundled 145, the decisive evidence is the rejected node ID and the actual volume, internal energy and level height passed to the inventory constructor. At the cold stalls it is the largest scaled equation, its trial and accepted values, and the reason backtracking rejected it.
3. For deadline-only cases the first-failure record needs the worker stage, elapsed simulated prefix, accept/reject counts and last rejection for that individual request. Per-seed counters include earlier successful commands. Capture these fields at cancellation in the next eligible lane. Changing tolerances or budgets from the current traces would obscure the distinctions above.

The audit used retained output and source at the current head. The watch worlds were not rerun. The raw reports are `tmp/junction-lag/perf-prepost/rolled-back-lane/head/campaign-{cold,bundled}-random-report.txt`, the isolated `tmp/junction-lag/perf-prepost/rollback-solo-145-941-report.txt`, and the earlier row diagnostics in `tmp/junction-lag/perf-prepost/recovery-lane/head/campaign-cold-random-report.txt`. The pre-junction reports in `tmp/unloaded-chunks/final-lane/head/` already contain all five cold line-search holds at the same committed ticks and the bundled-431 viscosity error at 1374.
