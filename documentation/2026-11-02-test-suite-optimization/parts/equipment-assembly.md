Scope: the 17 rows of `added-by-file.csv` under `science/equipment/`, `science/assembly/` and `world/`: 157 methods added since `f91c992d`. ## Sweeps that assert the same thing

- Yes: `skirtHoleTakesTheLinesSize` and the sight lines of `aTurnedBottomOutletTurnsItsPortAndSlots` (proposed above).
- Partly: `everyStorageSizeIsWalkedAndKeepsItsPipesApart` (20.0 s) and `storageDecksAndStairsAreWalkedInTheBox` state the same deck, ladder-start and stair checks, on the frame and in the box. The box adds climbing, rails and closed ends on six shapes, so neither implies the other. The owner rule in the first is its last loop; the walks should be the cost (not measured).
- No: `everyNozzleNeckAtItsLargestStandsClear` and `aColumnNozzleTakesTheLargestPipeItsPlaceClears` share shapes, but one asserts the largest clears and the other that the next step does not. `everyLane…` and `everyFlange…` share frames, but one checks the pipe body and the other the flanges.
- Older than this pass: `ShapeRasterizerTest.nozzlesEndOnBlockFacesWithRoomForAPipe`, `ProcessUnitTest.everyNozzleLeavesAFreeBlockForItsPipe` and `ColumnStructureTest.everyNozzleLeavesAFreeBlockForItsPipe` repeat `pipesApart` and the free block where their shapes overlap.
