Status: **Implemented 2026-11-09**, merged as 0.23.1. Branch `claude/modest-montalcini-bad40d`, cut from `main` `b794e2d41` (0.22.0) and fast-forwarded to `main` `f1697692c` (0.23.0) on 2026-11-09; 0.23.0 changes no equipment code and not the column rig, and the five failures are the same there.

## Scope

The final lane of `claude/refinery-compounds-thermos-252973` (2026-11-09) found five failures that `main` 0.22.0 already has; a scratch worktree on `main` reproduced all five. This batch fixes them on a branch of its own, so the 0.23.0 merge does not wait.

## Findings and fixes (physics first)

1. `PaintTest.bodyAndStructureMaterialsArePaintedTheRestKeepTheirColours`, `SERVICE_CASE`. Expected: a service case belongs to its equipment and takes its paint, as a motor's terminal box does. `616fe7a1c` made `SERVICE_CASE` and `SERVICE_COVER` BODY-painted (the code guide records it), and their sprites are neutral greys (mean 210-218 against the 236 a tint expects). The test predates it. Fix: the test's body set names both. No code change.
2. `ProcessUnitTest.everyShaftEndMeetsAFreeBlockForItsShaft`, GENERATOR 355 kW. Expected: a machine's block box holds every part it draws. `616fe7a1c` replaced the drawn terminal box of a low-voltage motor and generator with a fixed 0.4 x 0.3 x 0.3 m service case. It kept the large machines' envelope (`framePoints`) but not the low-voltage ones'. The case is drawn after the box is set, so on 20 sizes it stands 0.002-0.137 m above the box: the part above the box is cut off, and the 355 and 400 kW generators' terminal cell (y 2.001) falls outside the layout. Fix: `ProcessUnit.serviceCase` adds the case's top corners to the frame points; `EquipmentServices.CASE_*` and `caseFloor` give the case one definition. Effect: motors 30-200 kW and generators 75-400 kW grow from 2 to 3 blocks tall (box only; shaft, coupling, nozzles and terminal cells stay). Pre-616fe7a1c the 200 kW motor and the 200-400 kW generators were already 3 tall. None of these sizes is in `EquipmentLayoutGoldenTest`'s table (min, mid, max): the table is unchanged (0 of 96 lines, checked with a probe on compiled classes).
3. `ProcessUnitTest.everyNozzleNeckAtItsLargestStandsClear`, two drums. Expected: a nozzle takes a pipe's largest, and other parts give way (owner decision 2026-10-21). The new pad cases ignored the necks: on the 0.5 m vertical drum (1 x 2 x 1 box) beside the SIDE neck, on the 1 m boot drum beside the BOOT_DRAIN neck. Fix: `EquipmentServices.necks` gives each nozzle's neck and flange room at its largest pipe (none on a column: its bores follow its place). A pad case must clear them; `fitPad` slides along the carrier, then tries the inward leg faces, then slides up or down the carrier, down to a 0.2 x 0.16 x 0.1 m junction box. Where nothing clears, the case stays (the gate reports it). Only the two failing shapes' cases move (758 shapes of the three gate sets compared before and after); the 0.5 m drum's case goes to y 0.92-1.08, between its 0.8 m side necks and the vapour outlet. Golden table unchanged.
4. `ColumnOperationGameTests.aColumnRunsInItsPlantStopsAndLeaves`: "No room for the PUMP's creative motor". Expected: a rig builds on clear ground. The rig stands 64 blocks north of the test's own cell and assumed nothing stands there. GameTest slots advance across batches and earlier structures stay. With 73 tests the column test's slot is in a later row (grid-relative z 42), so the plant's box (z -54 to 14) covered row 0. The third pumparound pump's shaft block (rig-relative (-6, 1, 27)) fell on another test's blocks. The same offsets appear in all three logged runs, so this is not world reuse. Reserving the drive cells could not help. `Plant.removed()` also waits on every registration inside the box, so the cleanup then hung on the other test's devices. Fix: `ColumnOperationReviewWorld.rig` moves north a plant's depth (69 blocks) at a time, up to 16 times, until the WORLD_SURFACE height is the ground's in every column of the box.
5. `FluidHarnessGameTests.registeredPipesAndReservoirsKeepSimulatingAcrossUnloadedChunks`. Expected: every test removes what it registers, so the world is empty when this test checks it. With fix 4 the column plant leaves, but one island stays: island 371, nitrogen only, in the run's saved world at its end (`icheme_fluid_core.dat`). Its three device positions lie 1-12 blocks from the `cooling-tower` test's slot. Slots follow batch order, 8 tests to a row, 6 by 7 blocks; the mapping matches the logged drum of `equipment-compat-blocked`. `CoolingTowerGameTests.parallelCellsRegisterAsOneEmptyOpenBasin` placed a two-cell bank, checked its basin's registration and succeeded without removing it; the open basin stayed a live island. The property-reload test held it with its own (two HELD islands; a green 0.20.0 run shows one). Fix: the test removes the bank (`EquipmentTestCleanup.remove`) and waits for the registration to leave.
6. `ShapeRasterizerTest.surfacePiecesAddUpToTheFacetedArea`, the 1 m boot drum, 0.0046 m² short (found by the first test run on this batch). Expected: a bracket roots on its carrier behind the case. The case fix 3 moved sits on a narrow saddle edge. 0.09 m to the side the ray misses that edge and `rayNear` takes a face 0.35 m out, in front of the case; the bar ran back through the case and 0.007 m out of the 1-block box, and the clip dropped that piece. Fix: `EquipmentServices.brackets` treats a root in front of the case's back as no root (the existing fallback: up or down the carrier, else the anchor's plane), and keeps the bracket offsets on a small junction box. Every service polygon of the 758 gated shapes now lies in its box (main: 20 shapes outside, the motor/generator cases that fix 2 holds). 14 other shapes (columns, drums, heaters) had bars into their cases; their bars change, the cases do not, and the golden table is unchanged. All `ShapeRasterizerTest.SHAPES` pass the area check by probe; on main 25 shapes of `everySize` miss area (20 motor/generator, 5 natural-draft towers, which no gate checks).
