## 1. Expected behaviour (physics first)

- 651, slice 905..969 (3.2 s): LNG at 2766 kPa, 149.9 K, 2.76 % CO2 feeds 78.6 kg/s through a 50 mm line; 22 kg/s fills a 1 m3 tank of 100 kg with 2.9 kg of crystal, residence 4.5 s [M]. The tank rises 618 -> 820 kPa, 63 kPa/s [M]. A 5 % state cap needs about 10 steps of 0.06-0.7 s [M]. No node has to cross a phase boundary: junction j1 stays gas, 1.5 K above its dew point at 543 kPa, 138 K [M].
- 768, slice 5136..5236 (5 s): a liquid-full 1 m3 tank (457 kg, 39.5 kg crystal) loses pressure, 701 -> 242 kPa in 0.44 s, until it reaches its bubble point at 123.55 K and vapour appears [M]. This is the one real phase crossing: about 24 steps of 0.02 s at 10 ms each, then doubling steps to 2.1 s [M]. About 45 substeps in all [I].
- Junction buffers are algebraic at this scale: j1 holds 14 g at 3.44 kg/s, turnover 240/s, 4 ms [M]. A closure is slow. Particles need a fall across the bore: D/v_s with v_s = vmin/10 = 0.1 m/s [R, suspensionMultiplier 10] is 0.4-1 s in 50-100 mm [I]. A trace blockage is far slower: V_line/(phi Q) = 3e8 s for 768's e6 (phi 1e-8, 3.4e-5 m3/s, 1e-4 m3) [M]. Nothing physical needs the closure instant to 1 us. So the substep count of both jobs should be the state cap's: 10 and 45. The rest is method.
