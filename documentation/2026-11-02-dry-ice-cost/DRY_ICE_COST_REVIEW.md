## 1. Expected behaviour (physics first)

- The generators draw LNG at 110-170 K with 0.5-5 % CO2 [R, HoldFuzzTest:118-120]; the model's own CO2 saturation is 6e-4 at 118 K and 1.3e-2 at 153 K [M] (literature about 0.1 % at 120 K, 1 % at 150 K), so the LNG is 2x to 40x supersaturated. In a real plant such LNG frosts walls and let-down valves within seconds, which is why feed gas is treated to about 50 ppm CO2 before liquefaction. D23 and D61 are the game's simplification of that: a fitting's TP/PH decision never books a crystal, crystals form only at a vessel's refresh (`VesselCrystals.settle`) and melt on arrival at a junction [R]. Under this rule the state a junction or step carries is one supersaturated liquid (plus a vapour), every amount conserved, and a flash that only has to state that liquid should cost what a two-phase fluid flash costs: 0.02 to 0.6 ms on this package [M].
- The budget is per job: a 100-tick slice of an 8-14 node island takes 3 to 76 substep attempts and 10 to 188 implicit solves, each rate seed one balance start of about 24 junction-mixture flashes and each substep one phase check per junction [M, counters]; at fluid-only cost the flashes of the heaviest jobs are 0.1-0.6 s against 2 s.

- Proposal (a), an engine mode, not built, for the owner to validate.
